# Umbral

The Umbral, also referred to as the Umbral Order, was an ancient religious order of Synth Flux-wielders devoted to the shadow current of the Synth Flux. Driven by their raw emotions, including hate, anger, and greed, the Umbral were deceptive and obsessed with gaining power no matter the cost. The order had many forms until it reached the apex of its power under Xath Vhorn, the Dark Lord of the Umbral who achieved his order's goal of galactic conquest after a millennium of plotting. Within a generation, however, the deaths of Xath Vhorn and Xarn Velgor marked the end of the Umbral Lords. Though the Umbral ultimately went extinct with Vhorn's demise, they still had a powerful legacy, and many continued to follow their teachings. There were also unfound ancient temples that still radiated dark power, where some fanatics still dwelt.

Thousands of years before the Vat Wars, the Aegis Order was fractured by a schism that began when a rogue Sentinel and his followers turned away from the bright current of the Synth Flux, believing the shadow current was the path to true power. Having relocated to the planet Korriban in the Outer Rim Territories, their movement ultimately evolved into the Umbral Order, resulting in a war between the former brothers and sisters in the Synth Flux as the Sentinel Knights rallied to the defense of the Old Republic. Although the Umbral were driven off of the Core World Cintara by 5000 BRE, centuries of conflict ensued between the two orders, culminating in the Old Republic's collapse. Undermined by constant infighting and ruined by the last war against the Sentinel, the Umbral were rendered all but extinct with the exception of one; Darth Bane survived the ancient order's destruction and passed his knowledge to a single apprentice, Darth Zannah, before being entombed on Korriban, which became known as Moraband. Henceforth, the Umbral was governed by the Rule of Two, a new philosophy dictating that only two Umbral Lords—a Umbral Master and a Umbral apprentice—could exist at any given time. As a result, the Umbral were all but forgotten while the Sentinel focused their efforts on preserving the new age of peace under the modern the Concord.

The Umbral remained in hiding for a millennium until the rise of Xath Vhorn and Darth Maul in 32 BRE. Vhorn, trained by Darth Plagueis, hid in plain sight as Senator Sheev Sarnel of Serevin. Orchestrating the blockade and invasion of his homeworld by the Trade Federation, Vhorn maneuvered the Galactic Senate into electing him to the office of Supreme Chancellor. His entire chancellery would encompass the final years of the Concord. Over the next decade Vhorn sowed the seeds of discontent within the galactic populace with the help of his new apprentice, the former Sentinel Master Lord Karnel, who adopted the name of Darth Tyranus and became the public face of the Independent Movement for Self-Determination. The Separatist Crisis culminated in the formation of the Confederacy of Independent Systems and the creation of the Separatist Automaton Army which, in turn, compelled the Senate to support the establishment of the Grand Army of the Concord. Jar Jar Binks, acting in place of Senator Corolan, started a motion to grant the Supreme Chancellor emergency powers.

The Vat Wars began with the First Battle of Geonosis in 22 BRE, after which entire armies of battle droids and vat-born troopers deployed across the galaxy, making war and devastation commonplace for the first time since the formation of the Concord. In the final months of the Vat Wars, Vhorn sacrificed and betrayed Tyranus as part of his plot to turn Daren Nerith, the Aegis Order's prophesied Fated One, to the shadow current. Playing on Nerith's fear of losing his secret wife, Senator Amileth Corolan, Sarnel revealed his deception and true identity, while claiming that the shadow current was the path to immortality. Members of the Sentinel High Council, having learned from Nerith's report that the chancellor was, in fact, a Umbral Lord, attempted to arrest Sarnel, but the chancellor resisted. Torn between his faith in the Sentinel and the fate he foresaw for his wife, Nerith ultimately chose the Umbral by betraying Rasco Vel, allowing Vhorn to kill the Aegis Order's great champion.

Using the incident in the chancellor's office to deceitfully frame the Aegis Order for treason, Vhorn declared Order 66 was in effect, marking all Sentinel as enemies of the state and susceptible to execution. The vat-born troopers' programming forcibly turned them against their Sentinel Generals, who were betrayed and executed across the galaxy. With the majority of the order falling victim to the Great Sentinel Purge, Vhorn announced the creation of a new regime, transforming the Concord into the First Iron Dominion in 19 BRE. With the former Daren Nerith, renamed Xarn Velgor, at his side, the self-proclaimed Galactic Overlord's rise to power was complete, and the Umbral were restored to dominance after centuries of plotting.
