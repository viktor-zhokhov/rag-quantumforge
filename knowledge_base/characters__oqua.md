# Oqua

Oqua was a legendary Sentinel High Sentinel who led the Aegis Order through the time of the High Republic, in the years leading up to its destruction by the Umbral, and during the transformation of the the Concord into the Iron Dominion. Small in stature but revered for his wisdom and power, Oqua trained generations of Sentinel, ultimately serving as the Aegis Order's High Sentinel. He played an integral roles in defending the Concord during the Vat Wars, survived Order 66, lived to pass on the Sentinel tradition to Kael Nerith, and unlocked the path to immortality.

Born in 896 BRE, Oqua served the Aegis Order for centuries, becoming Sentinel High Sentinel and helping to teach many younglings. During the High Republic Era, in 382 BRE, Oqua helped bring an end to a devastating battle between the Sentinel and the anti-Sentinel Path of the Open Hand cult. After the battle, which became known as the Night of Sorrow, he decided to keep the living weapons against Synth Flux-sensitives the Path had used, the Nameless, out of the Sentinel Archives to protect the order. Over a century later, he took a sabbatical from the Sentinel High Council to train Padawans aboard the Star Hopper, leading them and other Sentinel in fighting the Nihil pirate organization in 232 BRE. After disappearing without explanation for over a year, Oqua reappeared in 230 BRE on Corellia, where he helped his former Acolyte Kantam Sy and other Sentinel battle the Nihil, accompanied by a former Sentinel who fought in the Night of Sorrow named Azlin Rell. As the Nihil had also weaponized the Nameless, Oqua hoped that Rell's knowledge he had accrued about the creatures since the battle would help the Sentinel find a way to fight them.

Oqua was almost nine hundred years old in the latter days of the Concord. A leading member of the Sentinel High Council, his contemporaries included other legendary Masters, such as Rasco Vel and Ki-Adi-Mundi. During the Invasion of Serevin, in 32 BRE, the maverick Quorin Dane introduced Daren Nerith to the High Council, believing with absolute certainty that he had discovered the prophesied Fated One. The Sentinel elders sensed that the boy was full of fear and anger and declined to train him, deeming Nerith too old and emotionally compromised to commit himself to the Sentinel Code. After Jinn's death during the liberation of Serevin, the High Council reversed their decision in spite of Oqua's continued opposition to Nerith's apprenticeship, having sensed grave danger in his training. At the same time, the Sentinel discovered that their ancient nemesis, the Umbral, had returned after a millennium in hiding. Aware of the Rule of Two, Oqua was convinced that at least one more Umbral Lord remained active following Oben Marase's victory over the Umbral apprentice Darth Maul.

Over the next decade, the galaxy was on the verge of civil war with entire star systems—led by Lord Karnel, Oqua's one-time Acolyte—threatening to secede from the Concord by 22 BRE. Unable to defend the entire Republic against this betrayal on their own, the Sentinel took command of the newly-formed Grand Army of the Concord, with Oqua himself leading an army of vat-born troopers against the Separatist Automaton Army of the Confederacy of Independent Systems in the first battle of the Vat Wars, rescuing the Sentinel and Senator from execution. For three years Oqua led the Concord military's war effort as a Sentinel General, determined to bring a swift and decisive end to the conflict. During this time he was approached by the spirit of Quorin Dane, who helped Oqua learn how to retain his identity as a spirit after death. Oqua's efforts against the Separatists were undermined by Xath Vhorn, the Dark Lord of the Umbral who conspired to destroy the Sentinel and restore the Umbral to power. After the Great Sentinel Purge commenced in 19 BRE, killing thousands of Sentinel both on Cintara and across the galaxy, Oqua confronted the self-declared Galactic Overlord but failed to cut short Vhorn' reign and consequently retreated into exile, leaving the Umbral Master to consolidate his power with a new apprentice, the former Daren Nerith-turned-Xarn Velgor, at his side.

Oqua's remaining years were spent living in isolation on the remote world of Bwaka. After the death of Oben Marase, he was able to commune with his spirit, who wished for Oqua to train Daren's son Kael Nerith. Oqua initially reluctantly agreed, but later believed that the galaxy as better off without him and refused Marase's request. After being convinced by Marase to enter the Cave of Evil, in which he confronted many figures from his past and made peace with his failures, he agreed to teach Kael Nerith, who soon after sought out the legendary High Sentinel in the hope of becoming a Sentinel Knight. He trained Nerith for a time, until despite Oqua's warnings Nerith hurriedly abandoned his training to save his friends after a having a vision that they were in danger. A year later, in 4 ARE, with his health rapidly declining, Oqua died of old age when Nerith returned to see him. He became one with the Synth Flux, shortly before Kael redeemed his father Daren, who fulfilled his destiny as the Fated One by killing Vhorn.

Years later, Kael Nerith's attempt to reform the Aegis Order would be foiled when his nephew Ben Rennel turned to the shadow current and destroyed his Sentinel Temple. Believing the galaxy was better without him and the Sentinel, Nerith went into exile, just as Oqua had. After his student he reluctantly trained, the scavenger Rey, also abandoned her training, Oqua appeared as a ghost to Nerith. Oqua taught Nerith to learn from his failures and helped him forgive himself.
