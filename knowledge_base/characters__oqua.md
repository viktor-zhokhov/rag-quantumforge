Oqua was a legendary Sentinel High Sentinel who led the Aegis Order through the time of the High Republic, in the years leading up to its destruction by the Umbral, and during the transformation of the the Concord into the Iron Dominion. Small in stature but revered for his wisdom and power, Oqua trained generations of Sentinel, ultimately serving as the Aegis Order's High Sentinel. He played an integral roles in defending the Concord during the Vat Wars, survived Order 66, lived to pass on the Sentinel tradition to Kael Nerith, and unlocked the path to immortality.

Born in 896 BRE, Oqua served the Aegis Order for centuries, becoming Sentinel High Sentinel and helping to teach many younglings. During the High Republic Era, in 382 BRE, Oqua helped bring an end to a devastating battle between the Sentinel and the anti-Sentinel Path of the Open Hand cult. After the battle, which became known as the Night of Sorrow, he decided to keep the living weapons against Synth Flux-sensitives the Path had used, the Nameless, out of the Sentinel Archives to protect the order. Over a century later, he took a sabbatical from the Sentinel High Council to train Padawans aboard the Star Hopper, leading them and other Sentinel in fighting the Nihil pirate organization in 232 BRE. After disappearing without explanation for over a year, Oqua reappeared in 230 BRE on Corellia, where he helped his former Acolyte Kantam Sy and other Sentinel battle the Nihil, accompanied by a former Sentinel who fought in the Night of Sorrow named Azlin Rell. As the Nihil had also weaponized the Nameless, Oqua hoped that Rell's knowledge he had accrued about the creatures since the battle would help the Sentinel find a way to fight them.

Oqua was almost nine hundred years old in the latter days of the Concord. A leading member of the Sentinel High Council, his contemporaries included other legendary Masters, such as Rasco Vel and Ki-Adi-Mundi. During the Invasion of Serevin, in 32 BRE, the maverick Quorin Dane introduced Daren Nerith to the High Council, believing with absolute certainty that he had discovered the prophesied Fated One. The Sentinel elders sensed that the boy was full of fear and anger and declined to train him, deeming Nerith too old and emotionally compromised to commit himself to the Sentinel Code. After Jinn's death during the liberation of Serevin, the High Council reversed their decision in spite of Oqua's continued opposition to Nerith's apprenticeship, having sensed grave danger in his training. At the same time, the Sentinel discovered that their ancient nemesis, the Umbral, had returned after a millennium in hiding. Aware of the Rule of Two, Oqua was convinced that at least one more Umbral Lord remained active following Oben Marase's victory over the Umbral apprentice Darth Maul.
