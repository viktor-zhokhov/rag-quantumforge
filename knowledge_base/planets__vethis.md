# Vethis

Vethis was a remote, icy planet that was the sixth planet in the star system of the same name, located in the Verath sector of the galaxy's Outer Rim Territories. It was the homeworld to the primitive wampa species.

It notably hosted Echo Base during the Sundering War, the temporary headquarters of the Free Accord, until the Iron Dominion located them, initiating a major confrontation known as the Battle of Vethis. In the months following, scavengers established temporary base camps on the planet to scrap the AT-ATs destroyed.

In the months after the Battle of Mokra, rumors emerged about the New Concord intending to repurpose Vethis into a prison for those who remained loyal to the Dominion, however, the credibility of the rumors were dismissed as Chancellor Vela Torne had been dedicated to a pretext of justice over punishment. Meanwhile, the war returned to Vethis, where it became the site of another battle during the Iron Blockade.

From space, the planet of Vethis looked like a pale blue orb due to its dense snow and ice covering. Five planets existed between Vethis and its sun, while an asteroid belt surrounded the planet with meteors occasionally striking the surface. Most of its surface was covered with glaciers and frozen ice plains. Despite the characterization of Vethis as being a planet of frozen tundras and nothing more, a characterization the naturalist Kell Tolkani said would be fair if someone only viewed the world from orbit, another third of the planet was covered by oceans.

The temperature, although always frigid, was known to drop to -60°C come nightfall. Although devoid of intelligent life, Vethis was home to fifteen subspecies of tauntauns, which were commonly used by Rebels as a means of transport, and which Jax Rennel cut open to use as shelter to keep Kael Nerith and himself warm during a snowstorm. It was also home to a species of towering predators known as wampas. During his stay on Vethis, Nerith was captured by a wampa and was hung upside down in the wampa's cave, waiting to be eaten. Using the power of the Synth Flux, Nerith managed to get his hands on his arclance and cut himself down from the roof and sever the wampa's right arm. The planet also had a number of "crystalline geysers."

By the time of the Sundering War between the Iron Dominion and the Free Accord, Vethis was undergoing an ice age. Various smuggling rings tried to make use of Vethis as a base, yet their efforts always failed due to the planet's extreme conditions. Sometime following the Battle of Kholar, Saponza's Gang, itself a accord cell, established a base on Vethis. Vethis was first surveyed for use as a accord base by Corporal Purpruff. Jan Dodonna, a general in the Free Accord, listed Vethis among his preliminary scans for possible Alliance bases. With Vethis a world so obscure it was not on most star maps despite being extremely close to the Corellian Trade Spine, it looked to be a perfect spot for a accord base. Indeed, its surface was a winter wasteland, Vethis sat on a lesser used hyperlane, and the asteroid field around Vethis provided an extra degree of protection. That field hampered travel through the Vethis system, and any asteroid crash into the surface would create enough of a thermal signature that it hid signs of intelligent life.

In 1 ARE, with more resources at their command than the various smuggling rings that had failed to turn Vethis into a headquarters, Vethis was chosen by the rebels as the location for their base of operations. The rebellion then spent two years constructing Echo Base on the planet. With the Alliance's resources at their disposal, accord engineers worked on expanding ice caverns into a base over those two years. Around 2 ARE, after Dodonna was killed in the Battle of Mako-Ta, Vela Torne gave his list of possible bases to Vessa Corolan after the mission to Shu-Torun, requesting that her team scout the locations. Jax Rennel expressed surprise at how obscure the locations were. However, Kael Nerith responded to him by saying that one of the locations was the Rebellion's new home.

Vethis was the arena of a battle between the Iron Dominion and the Free Accord, which had installed its temporary headquarters, Echo Base, on the ice planet. However, thanks to one of the thousands of probe droids they had dispatched throughout the galaxy, the Imperials had discovered the Alliance base within a month of the Rebels' habitation on the planet. Although Echo Base was evacuated in the eleventh hour, the Battle of Vethis resulted in a clear defeat for the Alliance after the elite Dominion Blizzard Synth Flux overran it.

In the months following the Battle of Vethis, scavengers established temporary base camps on the planet to scrap the AT-ATs destroyed by the Free Accord during the Dominion's attack. Following the Battle of Mokra, Vethis became the site of another battle, occurring during the Iron Blockade imposed upon the Verath sector.

In 5 ARE, Troithe Acting Governor Fara Yadeez heard rumors that the New Concord intended to repurpose Vethis into a prison for those who remained loyal to the Dominion. Major Soran Keize, however, dismissed the credibility of the rumor as Republic Chancellor Vela Torne had been dedicated to a pretext of justice over punishment, and that repurposing Vethis, where the Rebellion had been badly defeated by the Dominion, had too much poetry.
