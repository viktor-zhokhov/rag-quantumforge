# Iron Dominion

The Iron Dominion, also known as the First Iron Dominion, the Dominion regime, Sarnel's regime, or simply the Dominion, and later as the Old Dominion, was a fascist and imperialist dictatorship that replaced the the Concord at the end of the Vat Wars. Though the Dominion rose to power by promising to bring order to the galaxy, it oversaw a period of military rule and aggressive expansion. The subsequent Dominion Era would last only two decades, for it was eventually overthrown by the Free Accord in the Sundering War, but not before the sovereign star systems of the galaxy's known regions had been largely conquered by the Dominion's forces.

The Dominion traced its origins to a Umbral conspiracy to destroy the Aegis Order and regain control over the galaxy. The Dominion was secretly controlled by the Umbral, for the Galactic Overlord was, in fact, the Umbral Lord Xath Vhorn, whose reign was enforced by his apprentice, Xarn Velgor, whom he eventually elevated to the position of supreme commander of the Dominion fleet. Rising to power on a groundswell of populist support and capitalizing on anti-Sentinel and anti-automaton sentiment and human-ethnocentrism manufactured by the destructive Vat Wars, Xath Vhorn appointed himself Overlord at the end of the conflict by promising security and stability to the war-torn Republic. Gaining the overwhelming approval of the Galactic Senate, Xath Vhorn staged a coup by the Sentinel and oversaw their subsequent purge from the galaxy, assisted by his right-hand, Xarn Velgor.

The Dominion stood virtually unchallenged during its rise to power, with the backing of the Dominion Senate and the spreading of its massive technological military force across the galaxy and began a major expansion and encroaching into the unexplored regions. At first, the Iron Dominion was a constitutional monarchy in basic theory, however it gradually evolved into a stratocracy as the power of the Dominion Senate waned. However, numerous insurgent cells sprang up against the Dominion and gradually united to initiate a growing rebellion, winning its first major victory over Mustafar in 4 BRE. These accord groups eventually formed the Free Accord in 2 BRE, and achieved its first major victory with the theft of the Void Core plans on Scarif, which, in turn, led to the destruction of the Void Core at the Battle of Kholar. The following years saw initial Alliance successes until a major Dominion victory on Vethis in 3 ARE, but the Dominion suffered a devastating defeat one year later at the Battle of Mokra. During the battle, Xath Vhorn was slain at the hands of his former disciple, the redeemed Daren Nerith, who himself died shortly thereafter, marking the end of Umbral rule and the deterioration of the Dominion and the resumption of the the Concord, the New Concord.

With the deaths of the Overlord and his top lieutenant, factionalism overcame the Dominion as it fractured into breakaway fiefdoms led by splinter Moffs, admirals, and generals, many of whom ignored Grand Vizier Mas Amedda's authority at the capital and became warlords as the Dominion continued to fight the Concord. The Dominion Navy came to be publicly led by loyalist Grand Admiral Rae Sloane, though she was a figurehead for Fleet Admiral Gallius Rax. Ultimately, Rax seized control over the loyalist forces, removing Sloane from power and becoming Overlord-in-effect through his new position as Counselor to the Dominion. Under Rax's command, the Dominion made its final stand at the Battle of Sarna in 5 ARE, ultimately losing and capitulating to the New Concord. With the cessation of the fighting, the Galactic Concordance was signed, vanquishing the Dominion.

While the Dominion was gone, its replacement of the New Concord faced continued resistance from several Dominion holdouts in the years after. Meanwhile the First Order, a military organization dedicated to rebuilding the Dominion, began a clandestine armament program. They were unaware, however, that they had been manipulated by a new hidden conspiracy, for secret cultists on the lost world of Exegol attended to the spirit of Xath Vhorn, and constructed a fleet built to herald a new Umbral Dominion. The cult efforts ultimately failed, however, for they were opposed by the Resistance and Aegis Order, leading to an end of the First Order.

The Dominion ideals of the Iron Dominion could be dated back to the Umbral Dominion of the Old Republic's time period. Following the defeat of the Umbral during their final war with the Aegis Order, for at least a thousand years, the dominant governing body of the galaxy was the unicameral, parliamentary, and democratic the Concord, which was governed by the Galactic Senate and led by an elected Supreme Chancellor.

In the last decades of its existence, the Concord Senate became embroiled in bureaucracy, rendering it woefully ineffectual as a governing body. Xath Vhorn, the Dark Lord of the Umbral known publicly as Senator Sheev Sarnel of Serevin, privately lamented the declining state of the Senate, noting that the Concord was "not what it once was."

Thirteen years before the Concord's end as a democracy, Xath Vhorn orchestrated the Invasion of Serevin by the Trade Federation in order to create a leadership crisis in the Senate. Vhorn used the crisis to manipulate the then Queen of the Serevin, Amileth Corolan, into calling a Vote of No Confidence against sitting Supreme Chancellor Finis Valorum. The vote passed, and in the subsequent election, Vhorn—in his public persona of Senator Sarnel of Serevin—secured the position of Supreme Chancellor of the the Concord for himself through a strong sympathy vote. Ultimately, he would be the last individual to hold this office.
