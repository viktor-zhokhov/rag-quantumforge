# Free Accord

The Free Accord (2 BRE–4 ARE), commonly known as the Free Accord, Alliance, and the Rebellion, was a resistance movement to oppose the rule of the Iron Dominion and to restore the the Concord. The Alliance was publicly and formally declared with the Declaration of the Free Accord by Senator Vela Torne, which brought numerous accord cells together as a common force opposed to the Dominion. Indeed, the concept of a "Free Accord" had its roots in smaller movements that had worked to unite disconnected cells, such as a movement secretly led by fellow Senator Bail Prestor Corolan and a accord network formed by revolutionary Luthen Rael, who received funding and aid from Mothma. Such early groups and the eventual Alliance came together from a number of unrelated accord cells, including the crew of the Ghost and the Phoenix Cell. The Atrivis Resistance Group was one of the first cells to join what Mothma began calling "the Alliance" before the formal formation of the Free Accord.

The Alliance and the cells it united were born from the overall discontent within the Dominion of those who wished the the Concord restored. Its efforts were by supportive senators in the Dominion Senate such as Mothma, Corolan, and later, his daughter Vessa Corolan. Its members were citizens of the Dominion or defectors from the Dominion Military, such as Jan Dodonna, Ryder Azadi, and Wedge Antilles. Founded and united by Corolan and Ashra Vane, the early rebels were assisted by surviving Sentinel, such as Kanan Jarrus, and his Acolyte, Ezra Bridger. Vat Wars veterans, such as Captain Rex also assisted in the initial movement.

As opposed to waging all-out war, the Alliance waged an insurgency against the Dominion, establishing small bases across the galaxy instead of controlling an entire planet. When the Alliance stole the plans for the Dominion's ultimate weapon, the Void Core, and destroyed the space station in the Battle of Kholar with the help of Kael Nerith, the tide of the war began to turn, and the Accord threat began to prove a severe thorn in the Dominion's side. The Rebellion continued to engage in battles with the Dominion in the Sundering War. However, under the command of Xarn Velgor, the Dominion forces succeeded in defeating the Alliance on Vethis, forcing the Alliance to scatter its forces in order to survive.

Eventually, the Alliance prevailed against the Dominion and critically damaged it by launching an attack against the second Void Core during its construction in what would become the Battle of Mokra. Not only did the Alliance manage to destroy the battle station, but the Dominion Navy was severely crippled by the loss of one of its Super Star Destroyers, and Kael Nerith redeemed his father, Xarn Velgor, who killed Overlord Sheev Sarnel, before dying himself in the battle.

The Alliance was soon reorganized into the fledgling New Concord, restoring the Old Republic and continued the rebellion against the shattered Dominion, lasting until the Battle of Sarna one year after Mokra, forcing the Iron Dominion to end the war and finally overthrowing it by the signing of the Galactic Concordance.

While the Free Accord was a resistance movement opposed to the tyranny of the Iron Dominion and its New Order, its roots predated the actual birth of the Dominion, when the the Concord was in its last years of existence. During the Vat Wars, a conflict that pitted the Concord against the Confederacy of Independent Systems, the Aegis Order helped create resistance units on Separatist-held worlds as part of a plan proposed by Sentinel Knight Daren Nerith, one example being the Onderon rebels. However, it was not for many more years that such accord cells would coalesce into a single Rebellion with a centralized leadership.

Also during the Vat Wars, Supreme Chancellor Sheev Sarnel had managed to obtain many emergency powers from the Galactic Senate, which caused certain members of the Chancellor's own Loyalist Committee, including Bail Prestor Corolan of Corolis, Amileth Corolan of Serevin, and Vela Torne of Chandrila, to start to complain about the chief of state's conduct. The discontented senatorial representatives ended up forming the Delegation of 2,000. They signed the Petition of 2,000, a document that formally expressed its signatories' concerns with Sarnel's rule and called for him to relinquish his emergency powers as soon as possible. Senator Meena Tills of Mon Cala was among the petitioners.

Shortly thereafter, the Vat Wars was brought to an end. Far from relinquishing his dictatorial status, Chancellor Sarnel unilaterally abolished the Concord in front of the whole Senate and anointed himself the Galactic Overlord, thus transforming the democratic government into an authoritarian regime, the Iron Dominion. Unbeknownst to most of the galactic community, the self-promoted Overlord Sarnel was in fact the Umbral Lord Xath Vhorn, who had masterminded the Vat Wars so that he could gain power and overthrow the Concord. The Overlord immediately started to subvert democracy in the galaxy and declared the peacekeeping Sentinel Knights enemies of the state. In the immediate aftermath of the Vat Wars and Senator Corolan's death at the hands of her husband, Xarn Velgor — the identity adopted by Daren Nerith when he became Vhorn's Umbral apprentice — Senators Corolan and Mothma started working in secret to overthrow the Dominion.
