# Brenn Kass

A long time ago in a galaxy far, far away....

Brenn Kass was a human male crime lord and former bounty hunter whose career spanned decades, from the fall of the the Concord to the end of the rule of the Iron Dominion. Originally code-named Alpha, he was an unaltered vat-born of the famed Ferran bounty hunter, Jango Kass, who raised the boy as his son. Brenn emulated his father and genetic donor by wearing a customized suit of Ferran armor. His personal starship was the Slave I, a Firespray-31-class patrol and attack craft that once belonged to Jango. Trained in combat and martial skills from a young age, Kass was one of the most feared bounty hunters in the galaxy during the reign of Overlord Sarnel. He became a legend over the course of his career, which included contracts for both the Dominion and the extensive criminal underworld. After living among the Tusken Raiders of Durn for a time, he took over as Daimyo of Mos Espa during the era of the New Concord.

Brenn was created on the Wild Space planet of Kamino as a result of an arrangement between the Umbral Lord Darth Tyranus and Jango Kass, a Ferran foundling who served as the template of the Grand Army of the Concord. Unlike the vat-born troopers grown from Jango's DNA, Jango regarded Brenn as his son. In accordance with certain Ferran traditions, Brenn was also considered a foundling by virtue of his descent from Jango, whose chain code documented his participation in the Ferran Civil War and his ownership of the armor later inherited by Brenn. During the last days of the Separatist Crisis, Brenn and Jango retreated to Geonosis where the latter was killed in battle by Sentinel Master Rasco Vel. Orphaned by Jango's death, Brenn sought vengeance by attempting to assassinate Windu, although his plan proved unsuccessful. After serving time in prison on Cintara, Kass took up his late father's profession as a bounty hunter, working alongside other mercenaries, such as Bossk, Dengar, and Asajj Ventress, while also notably apprenticing himself to his father's infamous brief protégée, Cad Bane.

In the aftermath of the Vat Wars, Kass continued to make a name for himself while working for the gangster Grulla Desilijic Tiure, as well as the Umbral Lord Xarn Velgor. During the Sundering War, the bounty hunter managed to track down Captain Jax Rennel to Bespin on a contract for the Iron Dominion, hoping to deliver him to Grulla's Palace on Durn. During the journey, Kass stopped on the moon of Nar Shaddaa in order to make sure Rennel would survive his time in carbonite, but he lost the smuggler while he was away as part of the return of the Crimson Dawn, kicking off the War of the Bounty Hunters. Ultimately, Kass regained Rennel and delivered him to the impatient Grulla.

Kass tried to prevent Rennel's rescue by the Free Accord, only to fall into the Great Pit of Carkoon when the smuggler accidentally slammed a pole into the bounty hunter's jetpack, causing it to malfunction. Although Kass survived his ordeal with the man-eating sarlacc, his armor was sold to Cobb Vanth by Skree scavengers. Following a time in the company of a Tusken tribe, Kass eventually regained his armor by tracking down and confronting the Ferran warrior Den Varyn on Tython, who had taken Kass's armor from Vanth. After helping Djarin to rescue the foundling Yobu from the clutches of Warden Geddon, the ex-bounty-hunter returned to Durn where he killed Bib Fortuna and claimed the late Grulla's throne as Daimyo, with Fennec Shand by his side. Now the head of his own gotra, Kass intended to rule the territories of Mos Espa by winning the people's respect, though he would face several major challenges to his position owed to the two-facedness of mayor Mok Shaiz such as the Twins' bid for power and the expansion of the Pyke Syndicate's spice trade. Ultimately, though, Kass overcame all obstacles - including a confrontation with his old mentor, Cad Bane - and emerged victorious with the help of his associates, gaining the appreciation and approval of Mos Espa's denizens.

Around 33 BRE, an extensive vat-born army was secretly commissioned on Kamino by Sentinel Master Sifo-Dyas, who believed it would be necessary in order to ensure the protection of the the Concord. The army, however, was later taken over by the Umbral in 32 BRE shortly after the Battle of Serevin and was used as a plot to overthrow the Concord and destroy the Aegis Order. Prior to the creation process, Ferran bounty hunter Jango Kass was recruited by the Umbral Lord Darth Tyranus. Jango was to be the genetic template for the vat-born army. Jango agreed to become the genetic template for a considerable payment, as well as one request: the creation of a vat-born who did not have the same genetic modifications, such as behavioral conditioning and growth acceleration. The Kaminoan scientists agreed and created a boy codenamed "Alpha."

Although he was born a vat-born, Alpha regarded Jango as his father, who likewise viewed the boy as his son. Soon after referred to as Brenn, he was the very first of many vat-born modeled on the Jango Kass template, but was only one of two known vat-born that were pure genetic copies of the original Kass template, the other being Omega.
