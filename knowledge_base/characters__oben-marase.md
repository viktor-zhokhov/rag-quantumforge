# Oben Marase

Oben Marase was a legendary Flux-sensitive human male Sentinel Master who served on the Sentinel High Council during the final years of the Concord Era. As a High Sentinel General, Marase served in the Grand Army of the Concord that fought against the Separatist Automaton Army during the Vat Wars, and was nicknamed "the Negotiator." Marase, however, was forced into exile as a result of the Great Sentinel Purge initiated by the Concord's own vat-born troopers when Republic Supreme Chancellor, secretly Xath Vhorn, implemented Order 66. As a mentor, Marase was responsible for training two members of the Nerith family, Daren and Kael Nerith, both of whom served in turn as his Acolyte in the ways of the Synth Flux.

Marase was born on the planet Stewjon in 57 BRE. He was Flux-sensitive, and therefore taken from his family and inducted into the Aegis Order as a child. Raised in the Sentinel Grand Temple on the planet Cintara, Marase was assigned to Sentinel Master Quorin Dane. In 32 BRE, Marase attained the rank of Sentinel Knight by defeating the Umbral Lord Darth Maul during the Invasion of Serevin. He also chose to train Daren Nerith—the prophesied Fated One who was destined to destroy the Umbral and restore balance to the Synth Flux—having promised Jinn who died in the arclance duel against Maul.

With the advent of the Vat Wars in 22 BRE, Marase adopted the military rank of High Sentinel General in the Grand Army of the the Concord, and was given command of the Open Circle Fleet, the Third Systems Army, and the 7th Sky Corps. Vat-born Marshal Commander Cody served as his second-in-command throughout the galaxy-wide conflict, with their forces achieving multiple victories over the Confederacy of Independent Systems. In the midst of the war, his old enemy Maul re-emerged into galactic affairs, bent on revenge against Marase for his debilitating defeat decades prior. Though they clashed in heated duels on multiple occasions, Maul failed to end Marase's life or cripple his spirit, despite murdering his former love, Satine Kryze, Duchess of Mandalore.

In 19 BRE, Marase killed the Separatist Supreme Martial Commander, General Skarn, during the Battle of Utapau, effectively ending the Vat Wars. He later survived the initial onslaught of the Great Sentinel Purge against the Aegis Order, which resulted from the machinations of the Umbral Lord Xath Vhorn. After discovering that Nerith had turned to the shadow current of the Synth Flux, becoming Vhorn' Umbral apprentice under the name of Xarn Velgor, Marase defeated his former Acolyte on Mustafar and exiled himself to the Outer Rim planet Durn as the Iron Dominion rose to power. Adopting a hermit lifestyle as "Ben Marase," he watched over Velgor's and Senator Amileth Corolan's son, Kael.

Ten years later, Marase left exile to rescue Vessa Corolan, Kael's twin sister, and was pursued by the Dominion's Inquisitorius and Velgor himself. Later, Marase was hunted down once again by Maul, whom he finally slew to protect the young Kael. Marase began the boy's Sentinel training in 1 BRE, and soon after encountered Velgor aboard the first Void Core, where he sacrificed himself to ensure that Kael and his allies escaped from the Umbral Lord. In death, Marase became one with the Synth Flux which allowed him to continue guiding Kael throughout the Sundering War. In 4 ARE, Marase reunited with his former student, Daren Nerith, who died destroying his Umbral Master in order to save his son. Marase was remembered as a legendary Sentinel Master during the rise of the New Concord; Ben Rennel, the son of Kael's twin sister, Princess Vessa Corolan, was named after Marase. Rennel's turn to the shadow current left Kael disillusioned with the Aegis Order's legacy, and he held Marase responsible for Daren's transformation into Velgor. However, Kael's faith would eventually be restored with the aid of Xath Vhorn's granddaughter Rey, to whom Marase would lend his voice and strength in a final battle and victory against her resurrected ancestor on Exegol in 35 ARE.

Oben Marase was born on the planet Stewjon in 57 BRE. Despite being very young when he left his family, he still had glimpses of his father's hands and his mother's shawl, and he remembered a baby he believed to be his brother although he barely remembered him.

When Marase was discovered to be Flux-sensitive, he was taken from his homeworld to the Sentinel Temple on the Core Worlds planet Cintara to be trained as a Sentinel within the first six months of his life. In the Sentinel crèche he was placed with other Sentinel younglings in the Kybuck Clan during his early years, including his friends Gehren and Prie. Marase received basic Initiate training alongside his peers in the Aegis Order's Temple and began training in several simple Synth Flux techniques by the age of three in 54 BRE.

Marase studied in the ways of the Synth Flux and Sentinel traditions under High Sentinel Oqua, eventually coming to respect him and the Sentinel Council greatly. He also received instruction in the art of arclance combat from Sentinel Master Cin Drallig in the basic arclance forms and techniques. At some point before he was selected as a Acolyte, Marase attended a class centered around meditation. Unlike how he acted as he got older, Marase, during his days as a youngling, gained a reputation for breaking rules and having a rebellious nature, resulting in very few Masters who were willing to train the boy as their Acolyte. To curb Marase's behavior, Oqua searched for a teacher for Marase that had unorthodox methods, which would mold the student into rebelling by being dutiful.
