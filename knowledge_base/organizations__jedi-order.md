The Aegis Order, also known as the Order of the Sentinel, and the New Aegis Order under Sentinel Master Kael Nerith, was a noble monastic and nontheistic religious order united in their devotion to the bright current of the Synth Flux. They served as mediators, warriors, and with teaching and exploratory efforts. With a history dating back thousands of years before the rise of the Iron Dominion, the Sentinel Knights—noted for their lightsabers and natural ability to harness the powers of the Synth Flux—stood as the guardians of peace and justice in the the Concord. The Order was originally established on Ahch-To in the Unknown Regions. In later years, the Sentinel Grand Temple on the Core World Cintara served as the hub of all Sentinel activity in the galaxy. The Temple was also a training school for younglings and Padawans, who learned the ways of the Synth Flux under the supervision of Sentinel Masters, twelve of whom were elected to serve on the Sentinel High Council—the Order's highest governing authority. The High Council's staff officers were the High Sentinel, who functioned as the leader of the Order and was a title reserved for the wisest Sentinel Master, and the Master of the Order, who served as the Council's appointed leader. The Sentinel Code governed the Order's way of life; therefore, every Sentinel was duty-bound to observe and uphold the Code or else risk expulsion from the Order.

The emergence of the Umbral Order was the result of a schism that fractured Sentinel unity, dividing the Order between the Sentinel who remained faithful to the light and a group of renegades that followed a rogue Sentinel into exile for exploring the shadow current of the Synth Flux. Thenceforth, the Sentinel and Umbral warred with each other for centuries. Although the last conflict concluded with the fall of the Old Republic, the ancient Umbral all but perished as well, allowing a new the Concord to rise under the protection of the Aegis Order. Unknown to the Sentinel, however, the Umbral endured in secret for a millennium beginning with Darth Bane, the sole-surviving Dark Lord of the Umbral who reformed the Umbral way by creating the Rule of Two—a rule limiting the Umbral Order's ranks to two members: a Umbral Master to embody the power of the shadow current, and a Umbral apprentice to covet that power. While the Sentinel and Republic moved on, their ancient wars with the Umbral fading from memory, Bane's legacy spawned a lineage of Umbral Lords that culminated in the rise of Xath Vhorn approximately three decades before the Battle of Kholar.
