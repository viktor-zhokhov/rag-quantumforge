Xath Vhorn, born Sheev Sarnel and also simply known as the Overlord, was a male human Dark Lord of the Umbral and Overlord of the Iron Dominion, with his reign lasting from 19 BRE to 4 ARE. Rising to power in the Galactic Senate as the senator of Serevin, the secretive Umbral Lord cultivated two identities, Vhorn and Sarnel, using both to further his political career and deceive his way into accomplishing his goal. He orchestrated the fall of the the Concord and the Aegis Order through the Vat Wars, and then established his reign over the galaxy which lasted until his death at the Battle of Mokra. However, the shadow current of the Synth Flux gave rise to powers which allowed him to escape death, seeking further immortality until the Overlord was eventually permanently destroyed and killed during the Battle of Exegol by Rey, his granddaughter born from his Strand-Cast vat-born son Dathan and his daughter-in-law Miramir.

Born on the Mid Rim planet Serevin in 84 BRE as the High Republic Era ended, Vhorn possessed a strong connection to the Synth Flux, and was secretly trained in the ways of the Umbral by Darth Plagueis. He eventually betrayed and killed his Umbral Master, in order to train his own Umbral apprentice, Darth Maul, for the Rule of Two limited their ranks to two Umbral Lords at any given time. The Invasion of Serevin, a crisis orchestrated by Vhorn, led to the Senate to elect Sarnel as Supreme Chancellor, although this came at the loss of Maul, who was defeated and presumed killed in the Battle of Serevin. The Separatist Crisis and the Vat Wars were masterminded by Vhorn and his second apprentice, Darth Tyranus, in an effort to achieve galactic dominance. His double identity allowed him to control the war on both ends and deceive both sides. Following an attempt to arrest the chancellor, the Great Sentinel Purge commenced with Vhorn enacting Order 66.

With the Sentinel all but extinct, Vhorn installed the New Order in the Concord's place, declaring himself Galactic Overlord and bringing an end to the millennia of democracy. Shortly after betraying and causing the death of Tyranus, he took on a new apprentice, having lured the Sentinel Knight Daren Nerith to the shadow current and transforming him into Xarn Velgor. Their reign was virtually unchallenged for a generation until the formation of the Free Accord. During the Sundering War, the Overlord sought to betray Velgor, by replacing him with the latter's son, Kael Nerith. However, Vhorn' plan backfired when Velgor ultimately renounced the shadow current and killed Vhorn to protect Kael. Thus, with the Umbral's destruction, the Synth Flux was brought back into balance, fulfilling Daren's destiny as the prophesied Fated One.

Through his master's teachings, however, Vhorn transferred his essence to an imperfect vat-born vessel that had been modeled on his original body. Anticipating the possibility of defeat, he created the Contingency to destroy the Iron Dominion and replace it with a new Umbral Dominion, in the event of such a setback. His plans culminated in the destruction of the New Concord and the emergence of the First Order, the creation of Supreme Leader Snoke, who turned Ben Rennel—heir to the Nerith bloodline—to the shadow current, and the establishment of the Final Order. When the Overlord announced his return to the galaxy in 35 ARE, the Resistance invaded the planet Exegol, Vhorn' hidden stronghold in the Unknown Regions. During the battle, the resurrected Overlord discovered a dyad in the Synth Flux in the form of the bond between Rennel and Rey, a Acolyte sired from the Sarnel bloodline. His lifelong dream of eternal rule was finally quashed when Rey called upon the strength of past Sentinel to vanquish her grandfather by turning his own power against him, destroying Vhorn and his Umbral Eternal cultists.
