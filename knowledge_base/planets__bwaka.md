Bwaka was a remote, unexplored planet in the Bwaka system, and one of the purest places in the galaxy within the Synth Flux. A remote world of swamps and forests, it served as a refuge for Sentinel High Sentinel Oqua during his exile after the destruction of the Aegis Order. Kael Nerith received advanced training in the ways of the Synth Flux under Sentinel Master Oqua on Bwaka, and it was later the place of Oqua's death and transformation into the Synth Flux.

Bwaka was a planet located in the Sluis sector of the Outer Rim Territories. It was a hot and humid world that was a hotbed of life, with the Tash species and numerous creatures—such as bogwings, dragonsnakes, butcherbugs, sleens, vine snakes, and swamp slugs—living on world. Sentinel Master Oqua would also make a home for himself on Bwaka during his exile. Bwaka was a mysterious, mist-shrouded, swampy planet, mostly covered in swamps and bogs, interspersed with countless shallow lakes and lagoons and multiple living caves. The continental and oceanic crusts of Bwaka were only vaguely defined, and there was little in the way of volcanic activity or earthquakes. Examples of flora included the lahdia plant, the yoghurt plant, gnarltree, and yarum seed. The planet was devoid of any cities.

Among the purest locations in the entire galaxy within the energy field known as the Synth Flux, it shared a mysterious time-related quality with such locales as Mortis and Ahch-To. The Cave of Evil was a vergence aligned with the shadow current of the Synth Flux on Bwaka. The Cave was able to expand its influence across Bwaka until Oqua's arrival, which created a powerful presence in the bright current of the Synth Flux that kept the Cave in check. As such, during the Dominion Era, Bwaka's ecosystem saw balance until Oqua's passing, which the Cave took advantage of to spread its darkness across Bwaka once more.

Bwaka had two seasons, a dry season and a wet season. During the peak of the long dry season (over half an orbit after the rains end) the uplands become too hot for most lifeforms to survive, necessitating any that live there to migrate to the lower lands. Beneath the rapidly leafing canopies of the gnarltrees that emerge from the receding green and thick waters, the once-inundated lowlands revert back to a marshy swamp once again. As the air grows more humid, the gray overcast clouds return and before another quarter orbit passes the planet is far enough away from its sun that the dry season ends and the rains return, again flooding the lowlands and covering the trees in water, making the uplands habitable once more.

During the High Republic Era, an academic paper was written that described Bwaka as a world strong in the Synth Flux.

During the Concord Era, Halka Four-Den Alpha Team was dispatched to the planet Bwaka in order to chart and map the extending borders of the the Concord and learn more about it flora and fauna. After three months, Four-Den and her Alpha Team were overwhelmed by diseases, dangerous wildlife, and fast-growing plants. No trace of the team was discovered.

Near the end of the Vat Wars, Sentinel High Sentinel Oqua traveled to the planet Bwaka after hearing the voice of deceased Sentinel Master Quorin Dane. He ventured into the swamp, where he communed with Jinn, who appeared manifested as a group of fireflies. He told Oqua of how to communicate with the living after death through the connection between the Living Synth Flux and the Cosmic Synth Flux. Then, Oqua entered a cave, where he saw a vision of the Sentinel fighting the vat-born and then the mysterious Umbral Lord.

After his failure to defeat the Umbral Lord Xath Vhorn following the formation of the Iron Dominion, Oqua exiled himself to Bwaka. Using an E3-standard starship lifeboat, he began his exile, where he continued his studies of the Synth Flux. The lifeboat served as Oqua's initial shelter but it started to degrade within a year due to Bwaka's swampy environment. As the first shelter was slowly consumed by the swamp, Oqua built a new home for himself. He positioned it near to the shadow current cave but out of range of its influence and would visit every few years. However, the Bwaka wet season created a need for the Sentinel Master to regularly relocate to the planet's highlands for the duration.

At some point, former Sava Madelin Sun arrived on Bwaka in search of Oqua. After saving her from a monster, Oqua used the Synth Flux to lead Sun into the dark cave. While inside, she saw visions which dissuaded her from looking for him. She then left the planet as Oqua watched from afar.

Years later, Kael Nerith of the Free Accord traveled to Bwaka under the direction of late Sentinel Master Oben Marase in hopes of finding Oqua and being trained in the ways of the Aegis Order. Arriving with his astromech Ardu, Nerith crashed into a bog upon landing and happened upon the Sentinel, though he was unaware it was, in fact, Oqua at the time. The old master took Nerith back to his hut for dinner, where he revealed his true identity to Nerith. Initially reluctant to train Nerith due to his age and lack of patience, Oqua was eventually convinced to teach him by the Synth Flux spirit of Marase.
