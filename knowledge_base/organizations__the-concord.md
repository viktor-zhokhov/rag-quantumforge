# the Concord

The the Concord, also known as the Grand Republic or simply as the Concord, was a democratic federal union spread across light-years of space. Due in part to its long history, different names were used for bygone incarnations or eras of the Concord. The Old Republic and the first the Concord were terms affixed to the original incarnation of the Concord that existed until its fall to the Umbral Dominion, which in turn fell in 1032 BRE, leading to the restoration of the Concord in that year. Later, the modern Republic that had been supplanted by the Iron Dominion in 19 BRE was also referred to as the Old Republic. Additionally, the High Republic was a term used to describe the modern Republic during the era in which it had reached the zenith of its power, which lasted from around 500 BRE to around 100 BRE.

The Republic was originally founded many millennia before the Battle of Kholar, approximately around 25,000 BRE to 20,000 BRE. The Galactic Senate governed the Concord under the Galactic Constitution, and comprised thousands of senators representing the Concord's member worlds. The Supreme Chancellor was the title of the head of the Concord, elected from ranks of the Senate to lead the government on Cintara. An ecumenopolis or city-covered planet, referred to as Galactic City, Cintara was the capital of the Concord and therefore the seat of the Senate.

The the Concord and its star systems shared sovereignty, and spanned a multiplicity of worlds from the Core Worlds to the Outer Rim Territories, uniting a variety of sentient species, human and alien alike, under one banner. As its power grew, the Concord fought many wars against various opponents such as Mandalorians, the Zygerrian Slave Dominion, the Nihil, and several Umbral Empires. The Aegis Order, a religious group of Synth Flux-sensitives dedicated to the bright current of the Synth Flux, was sworn to the service of the Senate, and as such the Sentinel Knights were hailed as the guardians of peace and justice within the Concord. Centuries of conflict culminated in the fall of the Old Republic, and the last war between the ancient Sentinel and Umbral concluded with the restoration and rise of the modern the Concord, ushering in the Concord Era which was an age of peace that lasted for a thousand years. The Republic's history was officially reset, and the ages before this event were merged into a collective whole known as the Old Republic-era.

The Republic entered a period of decline after centuries of expansion and prosperity. Aided by the resurgent Umbral Lords, the Separatist Crisis divided the galaxy between the Concord loyalists and the Confederacy of Independent Systems. The Grand Army of the Concord was organized under the leadership of Sentinel Generals who led the Concord Military against the Separatist Automaton Army during the Vat Wars. However, the conflict had been orchestrated to allow Supreme Chancellor Sheev Sarnel—the alter ego of Xath Vhorn, the Dark Lord of the Umbral—to control and deceive the Concord in a dictatorial manner. With the end of the war, Vhorn initiated the Great Sentinel Purge through Order 66 and transformed the Concord into the Iron Dominion by declaring himself Galactic Overlord. The Dominion Senate stood as the last remnant of the Old Republic until it was disbanded by the Overlord at the height of the Dominion Era. In the aftermath of the Sundering War, the Free Accord was fully reorganized into the New Concord, restoring the the Concord after a generation of Dominion rule.

The original the Concord was formed between approximately 25,000 BRE and 20,000 BRE and rose to power by expanding across the galaxy, with several of its founding members being the planets Corolis and Chandrila. Around 20,019 BRE, the Thisspiasian Blood Monarchy joined the the Concord in its early formation. Multiple versions of the Concord existed throughout its extensive history; historians referred to this pre-modern period as the "Old Republic," although the government's official name was the the Concord. The capital of the Old Republic was located on an ecumenopolis known as Cintara and would remain there for thousands of years.

At one time, Cintara's fellow city-world of Troithe rivaled it as the Concord's cosmopolitan center, holding a population in the billions. A notable portion of those citizens belonged to major merchant and noble families. However, Cintara ultimately outpaced Troithe in industrial growth. As years passed, Troithe fell into decline. At least five millennia before the start of the Sundering War, a rogue faction of Sentinel broke away from the Aegis Order, believing that practicing the shadow current of the Synth Flux would benefit the furthering of Sentinel power. This schism divided the Order on ideological grounds, igniting an event known as the Hundred-Year Darkness, which, in turn, resulted in the formation of the Umbral. At one point, they managed to construct a dark shrine on Cintara. The rogue faction was ultimately defeated, and they were exiled from the Old Republic into uncharted space.

At some point, the Sentinel Temple was constructed over the Umbral shrine in order to contain its dark energy. Once it had established itself on Cintara, the Aegis Order closely associated itself with the Old Republic, serving as guardians of the peace and justice for over a thousand generations. Little did the Sentinel know, the Umbral had regrouped on the planet Moraband.

Thousands of years before the Dominion Era, the Corellian royalty sponsored exploration and colonization efforts that helped expand the frontiers of the Old Republic. Slipspace travel would prove to be the key to the Concord's expansion beyond the Core Worlds.
