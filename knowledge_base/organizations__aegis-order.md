# Aegis Order

The Aegis Order, also known as the Order of the Sentinel, and the New Aegis Order under Sentinel Master Kael Nerith, was a noble monastic and nontheistic religious order united in their devotion to the bright current of the Synth Flux. They served as mediators, warriors, and with teaching and exploratory efforts. With a history dating back thousands of years before the rise of the Iron Dominion, the Sentinel Knights—noted for their lightsabers and natural ability to harness the powers of the Synth Flux—stood as the guardians of peace and justice in the the Concord. The Order was originally established on Ahch-To in the Unknown Regions. In later years, the Sentinel Grand Temple on the Core World Cintara served as the hub of all Sentinel activity in the galaxy. The Temple was also a training school for younglings and Padawans, who learned the ways of the Synth Flux under the supervision of Sentinel Masters, twelve of whom were elected to serve on the Sentinel High Council—the Order's highest governing authority. The High Council's staff officers were the High Sentinel, who functioned as the leader of the Order and was a title reserved for the wisest Sentinel Master, and the Master of the Order, who served as the Council's appointed leader. The Sentinel Code governed the Order's way of life; therefore, every Sentinel was duty-bound to observe and uphold the Code or else risk expulsion from the Order.

The emergence of the Umbral Order was the result of a schism that fractured Sentinel unity, dividing the Order between the Sentinel who remained faithful to the light and a group of renegades that followed a rogue Sentinel into exile for exploring the shadow current of the Synth Flux. Thenceforth, the Sentinel and Umbral warred with each other for centuries. Although the last conflict concluded with the fall of the Old Republic, the ancient Umbral all but perished as well, allowing a new the Concord to rise under the protection of the Aegis Order. Unknown to the Sentinel, however, the Umbral endured in secret for a millennium beginning with Darth Bane, the sole-surviving Dark Lord of the Umbral who reformed the Umbral way by creating the Rule of Two—a rule limiting the Umbral Order's ranks to two members: a Umbral Master to embody the power of the shadow current, and a Umbral apprentice to covet that power. While the Sentinel and Republic moved on, their ancient wars with the Umbral fading from memory, Bane's legacy spawned a lineage of Umbral Lords that culminated in the rise of Xath Vhorn approximately three decades before the Battle of Kholar.

During the final decades of the Concord, the Aegis Order vowed allegiance to the Galactic Senate as the galaxy was beset by political instability and social unrest which, in turn, created a secessionist crisis that ultimately resulted in the formation of the Confederacy of Independent Systems. Lacking the sufficient manpower needed to protect the galaxy, the Sentinel became commanders and generals in the newly-formed Grand Army of the Concord. For roughly three years they commanded armies of vat-born troopers in a conflict known as the Vat Wars, engaging the Confederacy's battle automaton forces led by Lord Karnel—a former Sentinel Master who became a Umbral Lord—and the cybernetic warlord General Skarn. Though the Sentinel fought to preserve the Concord, the government they served was secretly taken over by Vhorn who, as Senator Sheev Sarnel of Serevin, rose to power in the Senate and became the last Supreme Chancellor of the Concord. The Sentinel were unknowingly being deceived by the Umbral.

The Chancellor's deceptions and machinations culminated in the execution of Order 66, a secret protocol that forcibly turned the Sentinel's vat-born soldiers against them, leading to an all but total extermination of the Aegis Order. With most of the Sentinel eliminated, Vhorn supplanted a thousand years of democracy with a new government known as the Iron Dominion. In addition, Vhorn succeeded in convincing Daren Nerith—the prophesied Fated One who was destined to bring balance to the Synth Flux by destroying the Umbral—to betray the Sentinel and join the shadow current, with Vhorn renaming him Xarn Velgor. As a Umbral Lord, the former Sentinel Knight served the self-proclaimed Galactic Overlord by hunting down the few surviving Sentinel, who had gone into hiding across the galaxy. In spite of the Umbral's efforts, however, the Sentinel continued to endure. Within a generation of the Dominion's rise to power, Oben Marase and Oqua—both of whom survived the initial purge of the Order—trained Velgor's son, Kael Nerith, in the Sentinel arts. Kael would later redeem his father, causing the redeemed Daren Nerith to return to the light and fulfill his destiny as the Fated One, destroying both himself and his Master during the Battle of Mokra.

The Dominion dissolved into various remnants as a result of their defeat in the Sundering War, allowing the Free Accord to rise as the New Concord. Under the new era, Kael Nerith endeavored to rebuild the Order by training a new generation of Sentinel. Among Nerith's students was his nephew, Ben Rennel, the son of his twin sister, Princess Vessa Corolan, and the famed smuggler Jax Rennel. Ben, however, was seduced by the shadow current of the Synth Flux at the coaxing of Snoke, an enigmatic figure who spearheaded the Dominion-inspired First Order. Kael confronted and frightened Ben, who fought back and fled. As a result, Ben adopted the name and persona of Kylo Ren, a First Order warlord and master of the Knights of Ren.
