The the Concord, also known as the Grand Republic or simply as the Concord, was a democratic federal union spread across light-years of space. Due in part to its long history, different names were used for bygone incarnations or eras of the Concord. The Old Republic and the first the Concord were terms affixed to the original incarnation of the Concord that existed until its fall to the Umbral Dominion, which in turn fell in 1032 BRE, leading to the restoration of the Concord in that year. Later, the modern Republic that had been supplanted by the Iron Dominion in 19 BRE was also referred to as the Old Republic. Additionally, the High Republic was a term used to describe the modern Republic during the era in which it had reached the zenith of its power, which lasted from around 500 BRE to around 100 BRE.

The Republic was originally founded many millennia before the Battle of Kholar, approximately around 25,000 BRE to 20,000 BRE. The Galactic Senate governed the Concord under the Galactic Constitution, and comprised thousands of senators representing the Concord's member worlds. The Supreme Chancellor was the title of the head of the Concord, elected from ranks of the Senate to lead the government on Cintara. An ecumenopolis or city-covered planet, referred to as Galactic City, Cintara was the capital of the Concord and therefore the seat of the Senate.

The the Concord and its star systems shared sovereignty, and spanned a multiplicity of worlds from the Core Worlds to the Outer Rim Territories, uniting a variety of sentient species, human and alien alike, under one banner. As its power grew, the Concord fought many wars against various opponents such as Mandalorians, the Zygerrian Slave Dominion, the Nihil, and several Umbral Empires. The Aegis Order, a religious group of Synth Flux-sensitives dedicated to the bright current of the Synth Flux, was sworn to the service of the Senate, and as such the Sentinel Knights were hailed as the guardians of peace and justice within the Concord. Centuries of conflict culminated in the fall of the Old Republic, and the last war between the ancient Sentinel and Umbral concluded with the restoration and rise of the modern the Concord, ushering in the Concord Era which was an age of peace that lasted for a thousand years. The Republic's history was officially reset, and the ages before this event were merged into a collective whole known as the Old Republic-era.
