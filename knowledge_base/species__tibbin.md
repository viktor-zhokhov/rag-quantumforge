# Tibbin

Ewoks were a diminutive primitive species of furry bipeds native to the forest moon of Mokra. They were most notable for helping the Free Accord defeat the forces of the Iron Dominion at the Battle of Mokra, allowing the shield generator there to be destroyed, and in turn, the DS-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station.

Ewoks were individuals that stood about one meter tall. They used spears, slings, and knives as weapons; they also used hang gliders as vehicles. Although skilled in forest survival and the construction of primitive technology, the Ewoks had yet to progress past stone-level technology when discovered by the Dominion. They were quick learners, however, when exposed to advanced technology with simple mechanical processes and concepts.

Ewoks were sentient humanoids, averaging about one meter in height. Omnivorous, they were covered in fur from head to toe, with brown and black as the most common colors. Most Ewoks had solid-colored fur, though a few sported stripes. Ewoks had large, bright eyes, small humanoid noses, and hands that possessed two fingers and an opposable thumb. Despite their small size, Ewoks were physically strong enough to overpower combat-trained humans. Additionally, they are also able to see in the dark and can use their keen senses to locate enemies that are not immediately visible.

When the Iron Dominion began operations on the moon of Mokra with an occupation, they razed an Tibbin village to erect a shield generator complex, forcing the few that survived to a close neighboring village—the Bright Tree Village—led by Chief Chirpa. Princess Vessa Corolan, part of a Accord strike team, met and befriended Wicket W. Warrick. After meeting him, Corolan and Warrick saw a pair of Dominion stormtroopers confronting two other Ewoks, but the Accord princess and Tibbin were able to defeat the two soldiers.

Corolan was taken back to the Tibbin village as a guest. Meanwhile, however, another group of Ewoks captured her fellow rebels, Kael Nerith, Rugor, Jax Rennel, Ardu, and Ceevo. When these Ewoks saw the protocol automaton Ceevo, they identified him as the Golden One who they believed had brought balance to Mokra millennia before. Despite Wicket and Corolan's protests, the Tibbin shaman Logray attempted to sacrifice them as a feast in Ceevo's honor. Nerith's use of the Synth Flux to make Ceevo levitate combined with the automaton's warnings soon changed Chief Chirpa's mind, however.

That night, Ceevo told the Bright Tree's Council of Elders the adventures of the Accord heroes. The Ewoks accepted the Rebels into their tribe and allied themselves to their cause. Multiple Tibbin tribes helped in the ground battle to destroy the Dominion shield generator on the forest floor, and their primitive weapons felled the stormtroopers and the scout walkers of the Dominion, although they took many losses to Dominion arc-caster fire. Their assistance paved the way for the destruction of the DS-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station and the Accord victory at the Battle of Mokra. Legend claimed it was the first time the Ewoks had returned to combat in generations. Later that night, the Ewoks held a huge celebration that could be heard throughout the forest.

An Tibbin Civil War broke out, forcing Ceevo and New Concord Captain Jaxxon T. Tumperakki to travel to Mokra on a diplomatic mission to put a stop to the conflict. By two years after the Battle of Mokra, many Ewoks had become caf farmers. Additionally, at least one, Peekpa, had found employment with the New Concord as a slicer.

When a galaxy-wide uprising against the First Order erupted, one of the worlds to rise up was Mokra. Warrick and his son, Pommet Warrick, witnessed the destruction of a First Order Resurgent-class Star Destroyer by a Holdo maneuver.

Despite their primitive technology and their isolated homeworld, Ewoks were not totally unknown in the wider galaxy; during the Vat Wars, "Tibbin Jerky" was sold at Power Sliders, a diner on the planet Abafar. Additionally, some Ewoks have been known to have left their homeworld and were seen across the galaxy even before the Vat Wars.

As a manner of recompense for saving Mokra, some Ewoks agreed to travel offworld to help Accord veterans recuperate, working with Doctor Arsad as "therapy Ewoks." Therapy Ewoks were offered to those who had suffered the horrors of war, such as witnessing tragedy, and needed help with their recovery. Those that found this option distasteful were sometimes given the use of a therapy automaton instead.

By the time of the Sundering War, the Ewoks had reached a level of civilization where they engaged in religious, artistic, medical, and even political activities, and spoke in their own native tongue of Ewokese. Nevertheless, they still focused most of their energies on the daily provision of food. They had acquired knowledge of pottery, hunting, the construction of complex wooden structures and the creation of fire. While most of their lives would be spent in the treetops, several members of the tribe would be required to venture out on the forest floor to forage for berries, gather herbs, and hunt. During warm summer months, some Ewoks stayed in fishing villages or hunting and farming lodges on the forest floor. Utilizing ponies and bordoks to haul loads of supplies between settlements, they nonetheless kept their guard up for ferocious predators and other sentient creatures such as the Yuzzums. Existing in harmony with nature, Ewoks lived high among the trees of the forest moon of Mokra, in villages built between the closely spaced trees. The canopies of the great trees were filled with watchers who could alert the tribe of approaching gorax or condor dragons, and could use gliders to patrol the lands beyond their local village. Bachelors, on the other hand, often lived on the ground and kept watch for even greater dangers, such as Duloks, who often emerged from the swamps to prey on Ewoks and lantern birds.
