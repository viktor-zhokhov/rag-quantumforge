# Sundering War

The Sundering War, also referred to as the revolution or the Rebellion, was a galactic power struggle in which the Free Accord waged a rebellion against the ruling Iron Dominion in an attempt to restore democratic rule to the galaxy lasting from 4 BRE to 5 ARE. The origins of rebellion could be traced to the Vat Wars, when accord cells were equipped by the the Concord and the Aegis Order to fight against the Confederacy of Independent Systems. After Supreme Chancellor Sarnel, secretly the Dark Lord of the Umbral Xath Vhorn, transformed the Concord into the Dominion and destroyed the Aegis Order, many accord cells began fighting the Dominion. A number of these cells eventually joined together and became the Free Accord.

The war had begun by the year 4 BRE and officially began at the formal establishment of the Free Accord in 2 BRE with Vela Torne's Declaration of the Free Accord. In 1 BRE, the Alliance scored its first major victory against the Dominion by stealing the plans to the Void Core, the Dominion's planet-destroying battle station, in the Battle of Scarif. The plans were brought to the Free Accord by Princess Vessa Corolan, with the help of companions, such as Kael Nerith, Jax Rennel, and Rugor in 1 BRE. An analysis of the plans led the Rebels to launch the Battle of Kholar, in which Nerith, with Rennel's assistance, destroyed the Void Core. A series of follow-up attacks by the Alliance, including an assault on the Dominion's primary weapons factory, left the Dominion reeling from the Alliance's advances. As a result, the Dominion hunted the Alliance across the galaxy under the leadership of the Overlord's right-hand, the Umbral Lord Xarn Velgor, in his new role as supreme commander of the Dominion fleet. Velgor's forces located the Alliance on the planet Vethis. The resulting Battle of Vethis forced the Alliance into retreat and scattered the Accord fleet. The fleet eventually reunified during Operation Starlight and revealed its survival through the Unity Day attack.

A year after the loss on Vethis, in the year 4 ARE, the Overlord allowed the Alliance to learn of the existence of a second Void Core as part of a trap to lure the Alliance to its destruction. The Alliance, believing that they were launching a surprise attack, assembled their entire fleet and attempted to destroy the Void Core. A large Dominion fleet lay in wait, and the two forces met in what would become known as the Battle of Mokra. During the battle, Nerith, who had learned that Velgor was his father, confronted the Dark Lord in a final arclance duel aboard the Void Core. The Overlord attempted to kill the young Sentinel, but, out of compassion for his son, Velgor threw Vhorn down a deep shaft and to his death, fulfilling his destiny as the Fated One and destroying the Umbral. Xarn Velgor, once again Daren Nerith, died from injuries he sustained during the battle, bringing an end to the Umbral's rule over the galaxy. The Alliance, meanwhile, destroyed the second Void Core, leaving the Umbral extinct and the Dominion fractured.

As a result of the immediate power vacuum left in the wake of the Overlord's death, many Moffs and other potentates began jockeying for power. Others began to break off from the Dominion, becoming warlords. In an attempt to prevent his people from learning about the Overlord's death, Governor Ubrik Adelhard locked down the Verath sector behind the Iron Blockade, sparking an uprising within the sector. As the grip of the Dominion on the galaxy and its citizens was relinquished, the Alliance restored the Old Republic as the New Concord and continued to liberate the galaxy from Dominion rule. One year later, the Dominion fought in a final stand over the desert world of Sarna led by Counselor Gallius Rax, only to be defeated. Facing severe internal unrest, the titular sovereign of the Dominion, Grand Vizier Mas Amedda, surrendered to the Concord. On Chandrila, Amedda signed the Galactic Concordance with the Concord, officially ending the conflict.

Although the civil war ended, Dominion remnants controlled by various warlords continuing to subsist. Meanwhile, select Dominion officers, nobles, technocrats, warlords, and other loyalists discreetly regrouped in the Unknown Regions where they established the isolationist First Order as part of a secret Contingency designed by the late Overlord Sarnel.

After a multi-year, galaxy-wide conflict with the Confederacy of Independent Systems, the the Concord emerged victorious from the Vat Wars. However, the citizens of the Concord did not know that their leader, Supreme Chancellor Sheev Sarnel, was secretly the Dark Lord of the Umbral who had orchestrated the war for his own profit and to complete the Revenge of the Umbral.

The war had opposed the loyalist elements of the Concord to a Separatist movement led by former Sentinel Master Karnel. The Aegis Order, despite its official role as peacekeepers, agreed to lead the newly formed Grand Army of the Concord, leading its vat-born troopers into battle along several fronts throughout the galaxy. The role the Sentinel played in the war caused their numbers to dwindle, both from the losses they suffered on the battlefield and from certain individuals, like Barriss Offee who rebelled against the Order for abandoning its beliefs. They also began to lose support among not only the political elite of the Concord, but also the growing military leadership of the Grand Army and a galactic population that they had become increasingly distant from. Protests against them and the war began, and, with their peacekeeping duties having been discarded in favor of the war, criminal cartels rose up to claim ground.
