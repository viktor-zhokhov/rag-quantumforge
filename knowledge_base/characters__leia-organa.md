# Vessa Corolan

Vessa Nerith Corolan Rennel was a Flux-sensitive human Alderaanian female politician, Sentinel, and military leader who served in the Free Accord during the Dominion Era and the New Concord and Resistance in the subsequent New Concord Era. Shortly after birth on Polis Massa, she was adopted into the House of Corolan—the Alderaanian royal family—and was raised as Princess Vessa Corolan of Corolis, a planet in the Core Worlds known for its dedication to pacifism. The princess was raised as the daughter of Senator Bail Prestor Corolan and his wife, Queen Breha Corolan, making her the heir to the Alderaanian monarchy. Instilled with the values of her adopted homeworld, Corolan devoted her life to the restoration of democracy by opposing authoritarian regimes, such as the Iron Dominion and the First Order.

Born in 19 BRE as Vessa Corolan Nerith, she was the daughter of the Sentinel Knight Daren Nerith and Senator Amileth Corolan of Serevin. Her birth occurred in the aftermath of the Vat Wars, which saw the Aegis Order purged and the the Concord reorganized into the Iron Dominion. With her mother's death in childbirth and her father's fall to the shadow current of the Synth Flux, Vessa and her twin brother, Kael, were separated to keep them hidden from the hands of her father and the Overlord. As the adopted daughter of a politician, Vessa grew up peacefully—albeit secluded—on Corolis until she was kidnapped by mercenaries led by Vect Nokru—under the orders of the Third Sister, a member of the Inquisitorius—in 9 BRE. Since he had fought alongside her adoptive father in the Vat Wars, Sentinel Master Oben Marase went to Vessa's rescue on Daiyu, and, after evading the Dominion's hand on Mapuzo and Jabiim, Vessa was brought back to Corolis. Years later, Vessa succeeded Bail by representing their homeworld in the Dominion Senate, though secretly she supported the Rebellion. During the Sundering War, however, Corolis was destroyed along with its inhabitants and the royal family by the Dominion's DS-1 Void Core Mobile Battle Station, causing Corolan to openly fight the New Order as one of the Free Accord’s substantial heroes.

In the aftermath of the Battle of Mokra, Corolan married the Corellian smuggler Jax Rennel and learned the Sentinel arts as her brother's apprentice until giving birth to her son, Ben Rennel, on the day of the Dominion's capitulation in 5 ARE. Her personal and political life suffered in the years that followed, with Ben turning to the shadow current like his grandfather before him, adopting the persona of Kylo Ren, and the Galactic Senate sidelining Corolan's career. Adopting the military rank of general, she led the Resistance and their efforts to oppose the rising First Order during the Cold War while seeking to make amends with Ben in hopes that her son will return to the bright current of the Synth Flux like her father during the Battle of Mokra. Despite her efforts, the First Order succeeded in destroying the New Concord, and Corolan's husband died at the hands of their estranged son in 34 ARE. Corolan continued to lead the Resistance during the war against the First Order, all the while passing on her Sentinel training onto Rey—a Flux-sensitive scavenger from Sarna and Kael’s apprentice.

As her health declined, Corolan used her remaining strength to reach her son, calling him back to the bright current. Following her death on Veandor in 35 ARE, her final actions inspired Rey and the Resistance to defeat the forces of the reborn Xath Vhorn's Umbral Eternal on Exegol and spark an uprising against the First Order across the galaxy. The heir to three inheritances, Vessa Nerith Corolan Rennel's legacy passed on to the Sentinel Rey Nerith, the military leader Poe Dameron of the Resistance, and Ben Rennel, her only child, who ultimately returned to the light before sacrificing his life for Rey and becoming one with the Synth Flux to join his mother and the Nerith family.

Toward the end of the Vat Wars, a galactic conflict fought between the the Concord and the Confederacy of Independent Systems that was engineered by the Umbral Lord Xath Vhorn, Sentinel Master Oqua set out on a journey to discover the secrets of immortality. His journey took him to Moraband, the ancient Umbral homeworld, where he faced visions of the future. At the end of his journey, the Synth Flux Priestess, Serenity, who had guided his journey, told him that there was another Nerith, and Oqua heard his voice from the future say so as well. These events not only alluded to the potential he would sense in Vessa, but to what Oqua would tell her twin brother, Kael, about Vessa in his last words, before his death.

Vessa Corolan Nerith was born on the asteroid of Polis Massa two days after the end of the Vat Wars and the newly established Iron Dominion's purge of the Aegis Order. She and her twin brother, Kael, who was born shortly before her, were the children of Republic Senator Amileth Corolan Naberrie, who died shortly after the births, and Sentinel Knight Daren Nerith, who had recently fallen to the shadow current of the Synth Flux. She was left with a lasting impression of images and feelings of her mother that her brother did not: that their mother was "very beautiful, kind, but sad", though Vessa was unsure if she had really seen her mother or if she had just invented the memories to fill the hole in her life.
