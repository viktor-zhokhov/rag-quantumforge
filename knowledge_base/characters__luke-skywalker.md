# Kael Nerith

Kael Nerith, a Flux-sensitive human male, was a legendary Sentinel Master who fought in the Sundering War during the reign of the Iron Dominion. Along with his companions, Princess Vessa Corolan and General Jax Rennel, Nerith served as a revolutionary on the side of the Free Accord—an organization committed to the downfall of the Iron Dominion and the restoration of democracy. Following the war, Nerith became a living legend, and was remembered as one of the greatest Sentinel in galactic history.

The son of Sentinel Knight Daren Nerith and Senator Amileth Corolan, Kael Nerith was born along with his twin sister, Vessa, in 19 BRE. As a result of Corolan's death and Daren's fall to the shadow current of the Synth Flux, the Nerith children were separated and sent into hiding, with Vessa adopted by the royal family of Corolis while Kael was raised by his relatives on Durn. Longing for a life of adventure and purpose, Nerith joined the Rebellion and began learning the ways of the Synth Flux under the guidance of Sentinel Master Oben Marase, whose first apprentice was Kael's own father. During the Battle of Kholar, Nerith saved the Alliance from annihilation by destroying the Dominion's planet-killing superweapon, the Void Core. He continued his training in the years that followed, determined to become a Sentinel Knight like his father before him, and found a new mentor in High Sentinel Oqua. After his master's death, Nerith participated in the Battle of Mokra in 4 ARE, during which he confronted the Umbral Lord Xarn Velgor, whom he learned was in fact his father, Daren Nerith. With Kael's help, Daren returned to the bright current of the Synth Flux by killing the Overlord and Dark Lord of the Umbral Xath Vhorn at the cost of his own life, fulfilling his destiny as the Fated One. Following the Battle of Mokra, Nerith trained his sister as a first Sentinel apprentice on Veandor, but Vessa ended her training after the birth of her son and seeing his death as a result of finishing her knighthood.

With the Umbral's destruction and the subsequent capitulation of the Dominion, Nerith became a living legend as he traveled across the galaxy during New Concord Era, searching for knowledge that would aid him in rebuilding the Aegis Order, and serving the New Concord and its allies where he could. As the war raged for a year after Mokra, Nerith was heavily involved with the New Concord's efforts against self-declared High Warden Ubrik Adelhard in the Verath sector, before defeating the madman at the Battle of Sarna. Around 6 ARE, Nerith committed himself to the liberation of the Vael and their homeworld Vaelis from beneath the heel of their enslavers. Later, in 9 ARE, Nerith rescued Yobu, a Ferran Flux-sensitive child that belonged to the same species as Oqua, from Warden Geddon's forces, and after temporarily taking the child as an apprentice on Kessic. During the training, Nerith was visited by Daren's former acolyte, Ashra Vane, where they discussed Yobu and how Nerith sensed that the child's heart was not in relearning the Sentinel path. After Yobu's caregiver, Den Varyn, visited the planet bearing a gift for Yobu, Nerith offered him with a choice, accept the gift and return to the Ferran, or take Oqua's arclance and continue with his Sentinel training. Yobu chose to return to Djarin, while Nerith bid Tano farewell as she departed for ways unknown.

By 15 ARE, Nerith had taken on Ben Rennel, his sister's son who inherited the family bloodline in the Synth Flux and had the potential for limitless power, as an apprentice. While both traveled to Elphrona with the explorer Lor San Tekka to study lost Sentinel lore, Rennel began to be groomed by Snoke, a humanoid being powerful in the Synth Flux. Upon the planet, the trio first encountered the Knights of Ren. In 21 ARE, Nerith started feeling an increasing darkness in the Synth Flux, having dark visions that frequently came to him in meditation, and undertook a quest to find the hidden world of Exegol. Nerith journeyed to Tython, where he saw a vision of Exegol and faced nine wraiths when the Synth Flux ghost of Daren Nerith appeared next to him. Following this, Nerith was able to defeat Kiza and the spirit of Exim Panshard but was ultimately unable to locate Exegol after failing to track down an assassin called Ochi.

In 28 ARE, sensing the extent of Snoke's corruption in Rennel and glimpsing a dark future, Nerith momentarily contemplated killing his nephew to prevent it, yet immediately reconsidered upon igniting his arclance. The rash action had devastating effect, though, completing Rennel's fall to the shadow current. The destruction of his temple and the deaths of all of his students convinced a haunted and regretful Nerith that the time had come for the Sentinel to end. Nerith therefore sought exile on the distant world of Ahch-To, having elected to live out his remaining days as a hermit despite the galaxy's pleas for help during the rise of the First Order. His solitude was interrupted in 34 ARE, shortly after the fall of the New Concord, resulting in a chain of events that led Nerith to play one last, momentous role in the conflict between light and darkness. Although he passed away into the Synth Flux, his legend would spread throughout the galaxy, rekindling a spark of hope in the enemies of the First Order. Before his death, Nerith declared that the Aegis Order would not die with him, having trained a final apprentice: the Sarna scavenger known as Rey.
