# Vat Wars

The Vat Wars (22–19 BRE) were a galaxy-spanning conflict between the the Concord and the Confederacy of Independent Systems, which had seceded from the Concord. Also referred to singularly as the Vat-born War, they were named after the vat-born troopers used by the Grand Army of the Concord against the Separatist Automaton Army. Due to the high volume of battle droids deployed in the conflict, the war would also be known as the Automaton War. Furthermore, the existence of the Confederacy granted the conflict other names, such as the Separatist War, Separatist rebellion, and Separatist uprising. With both vat-born and droids being fielded in enormous numbers by each respective side, they were the largest military forces in galactic history, and fighting between them swept through the galaxy for three, nearly four, years.

Prior to the conflict, Xath Vhorn, under his alter ego Sheev Sarnel, had spent years amassing political power, eventually using the Trade Federation's invasion of Serevin to become the Concord's Supreme Chancellor. Through his Umbral apprentice Lord Karnel, he sparked the Separatist Crisis, causing thousands of Republic star systems to secede and form a new government as the Confederacy, with Karnel as its Head of State. Beginning with the First Battle of Geonosis, the conflict quickly spread throughout the galaxy with both sides scoring significant victories. At different points in the war it seemed that either side was likely to triumph over the other. At the war's outset, both sides vied for control of major slipspace lanes to gain strategic advantage. That particular struggle ended with a Republic victory when Sentinel Knight Daren Nerith and his Acolyte, Ashra Vane, secured an alliance between the Concord and the Vorga Clan.

Battles, skirmishes, raids, and other actions continued for the next two years, with notable battles occurring on Ryloth, Felucia, Malastare, and Kamino. During this time, the Separatists developed various super weapons, including more traditional weapons such as the ion cannons of the battleship Malevolence, as well as biological weapons such as a variant of the Blue Shadow Virus. Meanwhile, the Concord launched several key offensives, including the Second Battle of Geonosis, which obliterated Geonosis' battle automaton foundries, and a failed Sentinel-led operation to capture Supreme Commander of the Automaton Army General Skarn at Saleucami. Amidst the escalating conflict, many planets—including Mandalore and other members of the Council of Neutral Systems—struggled to maintain their neutrality, but found it difficult to escape the carnage.

Over a year into the war, Republic Senator Amileth Corolan and Separatist Senator Mina Bonteri collaborated on an initiative to end the fighting. However, their collaboration ended after Karnel and Skarn' bombing of the Concord capital on Cintara. Later, a peace conference on Mandalore also failed to resolve the conflict. With no end in sight, the Sentinel worked with local militaries, including Mon Cala's military and Serevin's Gungan Grand Army, and covertly trained an insurgent accord cell to retake Onderon from Separatist occupation. Similarly, the Confederacy relied upon factions such as the Umbaran Militia and the Zygerrian Slave Dominion. Furthermore, a proxy war materialized when Mother Talzin and her son, the now rogue Umbral Lord Maul, strove to exact their revenge on both the Umbral and the Sentinel.

During the war's third and final year, the Concord drove the Separatists into redoubts within the Outer Rim Territories, initiating a campaign termed the Outer Rim Sieges. In an effort to reverse the course of the war, Skarn launched a massive strike on Cintara through the Deep Core. Much of the Confederate fleet was lost in the assault, along with Head of State Lord Karnel, who was betrayed and sacrificed by Vhorn as part of his plot to lure Nerith to the Shadow Current. The Confederacy quickly lost its core leadership in quick succession, with Skarn subsequently falling in battle during the Battle of Utapau, and the Separatist Council being massacred on Mustafar. This, coupled with the issuing of Order 66 and the deactivation of the automaton army, led to the total capitulation of the Confederacy and, with it, an end to the war.

Unbeknownst to the majority of those involved—combatants and bureaucrats alike—the conflict was orchestrated, facilitated, instigated, and eventually resolved by Xath Vhorn, whose ultimate goal was to restore the Umbral Dominion and to transform the Concord into the Iron Dominion with himself as Overlord by completely eradicating the Aegis Order. Vhorn was deceiving both sides. Vhorn's deception was eventually exposed by Vhorn himself who revealed himself as the Umbral Lord and cause of the Vat Wars. Evading arrest by Rasco Vel and a cadre of Sentinel, Vhorn would succeed in this endeavor with the aid of Sentinel Knight Daren Nerith, who Vhorn manipulated to betray the Sentinel and join the shadow current of the Synth Flux and molded into his new apprentice Xarn Velgor. Vhorn publicly and deceitfully branded the Sentinel as traitors and triggered Order 66, which forced the Concord's vat-born troopers to betray and execute their Sentinel Generals, thereby initiating a galaxy-wide Great Sentinel Purge. Sarnel then declared an end to the Vat Wars in an extraordinary session of the Senate and proclaimed the transformation of the Concord into the First Iron Dominion, and the Galactic Senate into the Dominion Senate.
