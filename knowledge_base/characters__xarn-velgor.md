# Xarn Velgor

Daren Nerith was a legendary Flux-sensitive human male who was a Sentinel Knight of the the Concord and the prophesied Fated One of the Aegis Order, destined to bring balance to the Synth Flux. Also known as "Ani" during his childhood, Nerith earned the moniker "Hero With No Fear" from his accomplishments in the Vat Wars. His alter ego, Xarn Velgor, the Dark Lord of the Umbral, was created when Nerith turned to the shadow current of the Synth Flux, pledging his allegiance to the Umbral Lord Xath Vhorn at the end of the Concord Era.

A vergence in the Synth Flux, Daren Nerith was born on the desert planet of Durn in the Outer Rim Territories in 41 BRE. He was the son of Shmi Nerith, a slave who conceived a child without a father. His blood contained over twenty-thousand midi-chlorians, surpassing High Sentinel Oqua and all other Sentinel in the galaxy. Quorin Dane, the Sentinel Master who discovered Nerith during the Invasion of Serevin in 32 BRE, theorized that Nerith was conceived by the midi-chlorians. Following the Battle of Serevin, the Sentinel High Council admitted Daren Nerith into the Order as the Acolyte of Sentinel Knight Oben Marase despite feeling that he was too old and emotional to adhere to the Sentinel Code. Nerith remained a Acolyte through the beginning of the Vat Wars, instigated by events set in motion by Lord Karnel. Although Sentinel doctrine prohibited romantic relationships, Daren Nerith had a secret wife, Senator Amileth Corolan of Serevin. During the early days of the Vat Wars, Nerith served as a Sentinel General in the Grand Army of the Concord, commanding the vat-born troopers of the elite 501st Legion against the Confederacy of Independent Systems. After receiving his knighthood, Nerith oversaw the Sentinel training of his own apprentice, Ashra Vane. By 19 BRE, when Tano walked away from the Aegis Order, Nerith began to struggle with feelings of failure towards his apprentice and the Order itself.

Following the death of Lord Karnel during the Battle of Cintara, Daren Nerith's faith in the Sentinel was further shaken when he was put on the Sentinel Council without the rank of master. He also learned that Supreme Chancellor Sheev Sarnel was Xath Vhorn, the elusive Dark Lord of the Umbral who orchestrated the war in order to take over the galaxy. Manipulated into believing that the Sentinel had failed him and coupled with his fear of losing Corolan in childbirth, Nerith betrayed and turned against the Aegis Order, believing Vhorn had the knowledge to cheat death. Consumed by the persona of Xarn Velgor, the fallen Sentinel Knight marched his legion on the Sentinel Temple of Cintara where they executed its inhabitants, including Sentinel younglings, as part of Order 66 and the Great Sentinel Purge. Velgor was then sent to Mustafar to exterminate the Separatist Council at the behest of his Umbral Master, who became the self-proclaimed Galactic Overlord. Velgor subsequently sustained severe injuries in his duel with Marase, and was rebuilt as an armored cyborg while his wife died of a broken heart, though not before giving birth to the twins Kael Nerith and Princess Vessa Corolan who were hidden from their father and the Overlord as the Iron Dominion rose to power.

Being the Overlord's Fist and later being elevated to the position of supreme commander of the Dominion fleet, Velgor enforced the rule of the New Order as the Overlord's Umbral apprentice throughout most of the Dominion Era and was thus seen as de facto Commander-in-Chief of the entire Dominion Military. In the aftermath of killing Marase in a duel aboard the Void Core and the subsequent Battle of Kholar, he discovered the existence of his son and was determined to turn Kael to the shadow current. Kael sought to become a Sentinel, like his father before him, and believed that Velgor had the potential to turn back to the bright current of the Synth Flux. Velgor was defeated by Kael during the Battle of Mokra in 4 ARE, but the young Sentinel refused to strike down his father in anger, causing the Overlord to torture Kael with Synth Flux lightning. The pain inflicted on his son awakened the part of Velgor that was still Daren, resulting in a redeemed Nerith killing Vhorn at the cost of his own life. Having destroyed the Umbral and fulfilled his destiny as the Fated One, Nerith made peace with his son and became one with the Synth Flux.

Daren Nerith was one of the most powerful Sentinel and Umbral in galactic history. The Synth Flux was especially strong in the Nerith bloodline, which culminated in Daren's grandson, Ben Rennel, Corolan's son who was born by the end of the Sundering War in 5 ARE. Rennel would turn to the shadow current as well, becoming the dark warrior Kylo Ren during the rise of the First Order. Like his grandfather, however, Rennel found redemption by sacrificing his life for the love of another—the Sentinel apprentice Rey, who heard Nerith's voice telling her to restore the balance, as he once had, during the Battle of Exegol in 35 ARE. Rennel's death ended the Nerith bloodline, although Rey, the granddaughter of Vhorn, rejected her true heritage and took their surname to honor their legacy.

Believed to have been conceived by the midi-chlorians, Daren Nerith was born to the slave Shmi Nerith. Although Nerith was listed as being born on the desert planet of Durn in some sources, confusion existed as to where Nerith was actually born; whereas some records reported his birthplace as Durn, others claimed he moved to the planet with his mother at a very young age. Regardless, Durn was Nerith's homeworld. While living on Durn, Nerith and his mother were originally owned by Gardulla the Vorga until she lost them to the Toydarian Watto in a podracing bet when Nerith was around age 3. Affectionately nicknamed "Ani," he worked in his master's shop, located in Mos Espa.
