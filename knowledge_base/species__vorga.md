# Vorga

Hutts were a large slug-like sentient species who were native to the planet Varl, but considered their homeworld to be Nal Hutta after abandoning Varl due to the Kayfoundo Kapa. They were well known as galactic gangsters who controlled the Vorga Clan crime syndicate. One famous Vorga was the crime lord Grulla, who ruled a massive criminal empire from his palace on Durn.

Hutts were a massive slug-like reptiloid species who had large mouths and stubby arms. They had three lungs. They were tough and muscular with thick leathery skin, which was wrinkled and slimy. Hutts often had watery eyes and slack facial expressions. Their tails were supported by a skeletal spine and allowed Hutts to propel themselves through muck which legs would sink into. Despite their legendary adult size, Hutts started out as tiny Huttlets less than half a meter in height.

Hutts possessed skeletons, and were not known for being healthy. Some Hutts suffered from a genetic defect that caused their skin to be bereft of pigment and prone to cracking. Whilst the Hutts were originally native to Varl, Nal Hutta—a planet with a hot atmosphere that was frequently streaked by greasy rains, creating a fetid sauna in which Hutts were most comfortable—later became the planet that was considered their homeworld. Hutts were immune to mind tricks.

Hutts could live for centuries—Grulla was 604 when he was killed—and could grow to enormous sizes. In fact, Hutts' lifespans could span over 1,000 years. The literary collective term for Hutts was a bulge of Hutts. Hutts spent the first 50 years of their life in the brood pouches of their parents. When they emerged, they were described as having the mind of an infant.

The Hutts originally hailed from the planet Varl, where they were hunted by the Kayfoundo Kapa. In order to escape the Kayfoundo Kapa, the Hutts ultimately abandoned Varl and settled on the planet Nal Hutta. For centuries, the Hutts dominated much of the organized criminal activities in the galaxy. They dominated the Twi'leks of Ryloth and the Nikto species for several centuries.

The Hutts approached Lord Isamer of the Directorate, striking a deal with him to destabilize the local governments of Eiram and E'ronoh to the advantage of their long-term plans for control of trade and mass enslavement. To that end, Isamer instigated the Eiram–E'ronoh crisis, but in actuality the Hutts intended for the crisis to destroy the Directorate, allowing them to freely move their criminal operations into the region.

During the chancellorship of Lina Soh, the Hutts remained quiet and were not known to venture into Republic space due to sanctions upon the Vorga Clans concerning open trade in Republic territory. However, in the wake of the Great Slipspace Disaster, a Vorga and their guards acquired a vratixia renanicus shipment from the Sedri system, and was passing through the Kazlin system when their starship was attacked by the Nihil, who killed the Vorga. The wreckage was investigated by Sskeer, Keeve Trennis, and the Kotabi bond-twins Ceret and Terec, and the Vorga's corpse was recovered to Starlight Beacon by the Ataraxia. Avar Kriss requested an autopsy, conducted by OrbaLin who discovered the cadaver was infected. The infestation was revealed to be Drengir, which burst from the corpse and attacked the Sentinel.

Meanwhile, the Vorga Cartel arrived on Sedri Minor to uphold a bargain struck with the colonists for protection in exchange for regular grain payments. Myarga Anjiliac Atirue discussed the arrangement with Kriss but was attacked by Sskeer, igniting a battle between the Cartel's mercenaries and the Sentinel until Kriss convinced Myarga to turn her attention to their common enemy, the Drengir, by broadcasting distress calls from across the Galactic Frontier and Nal Hutta itself. Though Myarga believed the Five Families would never condone an alliance with the Sentinel, she directed her warriors to fight alongside them to battle the Drengir. Trennis was able to convince the Drengir to retreat by performing a mind touch on Sskeer, communicating the idea that the "meat" on Sedri Minor was spoiled.

The uneasy alliance continued in the following months as Avar and Myarga slowed the Drengir infestations across the Frontier. The alliance ended with the defeat of the Drengir during the assault on Mulita; while Myarga wanted her forces to execute the captured Great Progenitor of the Drengir, the Sentinel defended her as a living being whom they had captured, shattering the alliance. The focus on the Drengir crisis had, however, given the Nihil marauders time to gather and launch the devastating Valo Atrocity.

During the Vat Wars, the the Concord forged a tacit alliance with the crime lord Grulla Desilijic Tiure after the Sentinel Daren Nerith and his Acolyte Ashra Vane rescued his son Rotta. The Republic Senator Amileth Corolan also foiled a plot by Grulla's uncle Ziro to overthrow his nephew with the collusion of the Confederacy of Independent Systems.

Later in the war, the renegade Umbral Lord Maul approached the Grand Vorga Council on the planet Nal Hutta to offer them a place in his Shadow Collective. After denying him, a force of bounty hunters attacked Shadow Collective's forces, but after being defeated, saw one of their members, Oruba, killed by Savage Opress. Eventually, Maul's forces would visit Grulla and Gorga on Durn and, after a short battle, convinced the two Hutts to join them.

During the Age of the Dominion, the Hutts and other organized criminal groups exercised a powerful grip on the galaxy. The Hutts also had extensive dealings with the Iron Dominion in return for the protection of Vorga Space. They performed certain important, but off-the-books, services, including helping to run several black-site prisons.
