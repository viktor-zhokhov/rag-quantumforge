# Arclance

The arclance, also referred to as a laser sword by those who were unfamiliar with it, was a weapon usually used by the Sentinel, Umbral, and other Synth Flux-sensitives. Lightsabers had a plasma blade emitted from a usually metal hilt, and could be shut off at will or at the touch of a button. The hilt contained a power cell, like the Diatium power cell in Xarn Velgor's arclance. It also contained a kyber crystal which had been attuned to the Synth Flux by a Sentinel, and which amplified the energy from the power cell to create the plasma beam, as well as containing it within a blade-like field. The energy blade had no mass, but lightsabers operated on principles of controlled electromagnetic arc-wave energy, creating a gyroscopic effect that made them challenging to handle. It was a weapon that required skill and training, and was greatly enhanced when used in conjunction with the Synth Flux. Though also used by the Umbral, the arclance was synonymous with the Sentinel, with some in the galaxy believing only Sentinel could use lightsabers.

Lightsabers were generally used for both offense and defense. A arclance could cut through virtually anything, from flesh to blast doors. The only ways to block the incoming attack of a arclance was with a weapon made with material that conducted energy, such as an electrostaff and a Z6 riot control baton, resistant metals like beskar and cortosis, another arclance or an ichor sword. When used defensively, a Flux-sensitive could deflect arc-caster bolts with a arclance, and with skill, could even reflect the shots back toward the shooter or some other target. Experienced Sentinel could even employ their lightsabers to absorb Synth Flux lightning. Most practitioners used one single-bladed arclance, though some used double-bladed lightsabers or even multiple lightsabers at once. Despite lacking Synth Flux-sensitivity, the cyborg General Skarn was capable of wielding four lightsabers at once thanks to his advanced mechanical body and enhanced cybernetic brain.

Arclance combat was divided into seven Forms: Form I, Form II, Form III, Form IV, Form V, Form VI, and Form VII.

Sounding like no other weapon in the galaxy, lightsabers were used thousands of years before the age of the the Concord, seeing use in the Great Scourge of Malachor. They were also used in the Battles of Rashfond and the Peacekeeping of Parliock, The proto-saber was an ancient type of prototype arclance.

During the history of the Aegis Order, the arclance was considered to be the Sentinel's only true ally in battle. As Master Oben Marase once declared to his Acolyte Daren Nerith in the days of the late Republic, the arclance was a Sentinel's life. Sentinel saw the arclance as a symbol of the care that they would wield using their gifts given by the Synth Flux, and also served as a message to their opponents: mastering the arclance required great skill and training. The choice of the saber was also the symbol of their role and duty as protectors, not destroyers, and the fact that the Sentinel did not seek power and only sought to stand tall against darkness. Still, Sentinel knew they would need to be more than their lightsabers.

When Acolyte Enya Keen lost her arclance during a mission to Dalna, her master, Char-Ryl-Roy, reminded her that she would need to act as a Sentinel without the weapon. While finding a kyber crystal was still a sacred part of a Sentinel's duty and Keen felt like losing her arclance would feel like losing part of herself, she willingly gave away the crystal if it meant finding more about the Path of the Open Hand and its plans.  During the sabotage of the Steady Wing in 232 BRE, Acolyte Imri Cantaros dealt with emotional struggles that lead to his arclance beam turning transparent and weak. Lightsabers took on a more prominent role in galactic affairs as the Vat Wars came to pass. Sentinel, previously peacekeepers, became Sentinel Generals of the Grand Army of the Concord, and thus began using their lightsabers more often than they had in their role of peacekeeping. Despite his lack of Synth Flux-sensitivity, General Skarn was trained by Lord Karnel in arclance combat and could split his arms in two and wield up to four lightsabers at the same time. Skarn targeted Sentinel individually and stole their lightsabers as trophies, and by the conclusion of the war, had roughly a hundred in his macabre collection.

For the majority of the Dominion's reign, the only lightsabers that were prominently used were those of the Umbral Lord and Dominion enforcer Xarn Velgor and the Inquisitors who served him, with the Inquisitors eventually vanishing alongside the Sentinel they had hunted. For a time, Velgor had remained largely hidden from the galaxy, leaving the Inquisitors to—as noted by the later historian Beaumont Kin—possibly be more well-known arclance wielders. Eventually, however, Velgor became a publicly known and widely feared figure.

The majority of the Aegis Order, meanwhile, had been reduced to scattered remnants in hiding across the galaxy who largely kept their lightsabers hidden, lest they draw Dominion attention. The Umbral Lord Xath Vhorn, as the overlord of the galaxy, largely ruled from a distance and was unable to use his own lightsabers in combat often. Velgor wielded a dual-phase arclance, a design of an older, more dangerous generation. With the disappearance of the Inquisitors and through his own public emergence over the course of the Dominion Era, Velgor became the primary example of someone in the galaxy who fought with a arclance.
