# Jax Rennel

Jax Rennel, known only as Jax until being given the surname Rennel, was a human male smuggler and hero of the Free Accord. He rose from an impoverished childhood to become captain of the Vagabond, a leader in the Free Accord and an instrumental figure in the Sundering War during the reign of the Iron Dominion. Although he did not believe in the Synth Flux controlling his destiny, Jax came to believe in the cause of galactic freedom, joining Sentinel Kael Nerith and Princess Vessa Corolan in the fight for the Rebellion. Following the war, Jax and Vessa welcomed a son, Ben Rennel, who became the dark knight Kylo Ren after being corrupted by the shadow current of the Synth Flux. Although their relationship floundered, Vessa hoped that Jax, as Ben's father, could find a way to break the shadow current's hold on their son. Jax Rennel became legendary across the galaxy as both a smuggler and war hero of the Rebellion.

As a young man fighting for survival on the mean streets of Corellia, Jax yearned to fly among the stars with dreams of becoming the galaxy's best pilot. With a fellow scrumrat named Qi'ra, Jax lived as a street thief with Corellia's notorious White Worms gang until an important deal with Lady Proxima went bad, causing Jax to flee Corellia. He was given the full name "Jax Rennel" by an Dominion recruitment officer while entering the Dominion Academy. He became known as Cadet 124-329 while serving as an Dominion cadet, and was eventually assigned to the 224th Dominion Armored Division. On Mimban, Jax met the thief Tobias Beckett and wound up deserting the Dominion and joining his crew to steal unrefined coaxium from Kessel in order to pay a debt to Dryden Vos, a kingpin with the Crimson Dawn crime syndicate. By the end of this scheme, during which he reunited with Qi'ra, Jax was forced to kill his mentor Beckett after a brutal double-cross. Jax also became rivals and close friends with fellow smuggler Vero Calnys and the Grovaki Rugor, the latter who became his best friend. Calnys owned the Vagabond, a fast ship with which Jax made the Kessel Run in just twelve parsecs and won from Vero in a game of sabacc.

With only Rugor remaining at his side, as his trusted copilot who swore a life debt to the Corellian smuggler, Jax Rennel embarked on a smuggling career under the employ of Grulla the Vorga, a notorious gangster and crime lord of Durn. After ditching a shipment of spice to avoid trouble with the Dominion, Rennel owed a great deal of money to Grulla, who then placed a bounty on the smuggler's head, as a result. His fortune seemed to have changed when Jax and Rugor were hired to charter Kael Nerith, Oben Marase, and the droids Ardu and Ceevo to Corolis, avoiding Dominion stormtroopers who were searching for Kael's droids. However, as they find Corolis destroyed by the Dominion's new superweapon, the Void Core, Rennel became caught up in the rebellion against the Dominion, rescuing Princess Vessa Corolan, delivering the stolen Void Core plans to the hidden Accord base on Kholar 4, and saved Kael from the Umbral Lord Xarn Velgor in the Battle of Kholar, allowing Nerith to destroy the Void Core. Rennel fought with the Rebellion for a number of years afterward, taking part in numerous operations and battles against the Dominion.

After the Battle of Vethis, Rennel sought refuge with Corolan—with whom he had fallen in love—Rugor, and Ceevo on Cloud City, which was operated by his old friend Vero Calnys. There, they were captured by Xarn Velgor as part of a trap to bait Nerith to the city. Rennel was frozen in carbonite and taken to Grulla's Palace on Durn by the bounty hunter Brenn Kass, where he remained encased in carbonite for months until he was rescued by his friends. After returning to the Alliance Fleet, Rennel was promoted to the rank of general and was given command of the Pathfinders, a special forces squad, for the Battle of Mokra. Rennel led the Pathfinders to Mokra, where he, Corolan, Rugor, and the special forces team, with the help of the native Ewoks, destroyed the shield generator protecting the second Void Core that was being constructed in orbit. Their actions allowed the Alliance Fleet to destroy the superweapon.

The Battle of Mokra, which saw the deaths of Overlord Sheev Sarnel and Xarn Velgor, allowed the Rebellion to form the New Concord. In the early days after Mokra, Rennel continued to lead the Pathfinders against the Dominion. He married Corolan, and the two had a son, Ben Rennel. The boy struggled with the shadow current of the Synth Flux—like his mother, who was the daughter of Xarn Velgor and twin sister of Kael Nerith, Ben was Flux-sensitive—so Corolan sent him to be trained as a Sentinel by Nerith. Ben was seduced to the shadow current by Snoke, Supreme Leader of the First Order, resulting in the destruction of Nerith's attempt to rebuild the Aegis Order.
