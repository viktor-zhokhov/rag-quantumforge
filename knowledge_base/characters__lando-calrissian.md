# Vero Calnys

Landonis Balthazar Calnys, otherwise known as Vero Calnys, was a human smuggler, gambler, and entrepreneur who became Baron Administrator of Cloud City and, later, a general in the Free Accord. Born on Socorro, he was the owner of the Vagabond before losing it to Jax Rennel in a game of sabacc on Numidian Prime. Afterward, Calnys put an end to his days as a smuggler and became an entrepreneur, setting up a small mining operation on the planet Lothal before eventually becoming the leader of Cloud City in the skies of the planet Bespin.

During the Sundering War, Xarn Velgor arrived on Cloud City to lay a trap for his son, Kael Nerith. As part of the trap, the Dark Lord of the Umbral forced Calnys into tricking a group of Rebels, including Rennel and Princess Vessa Corolan, leading them to Velgor himself. Though Velgor promised to leave Cloud City without an Dominion presence, Calnys felt the deal had been altered to the point where he could no longer tolerate it. Calnys alerted his citizens to the Iron Dominion's presence and impending occupation and ordered an evacuation. He helped the Rebels try to rescue Rennel, who had been frozen in carbonite, from Brenn Kass, but the bounty hunter escaped.

Calnys joined the Free Accord and set out to find Rennel. After locating him in the palace of Grulla the Vorga on Durn, Calnys aided in his rescue. The Rebels returned to the fleet, and Calnys became a general, volunteering to lead the assault on the DS-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station during the Battle of Mokra. During the battle, he piloted the Vagabond into the battle station's core, firing the shot which destroyed it.

In the years of peace after the Dominion's dissolution, Calnys started a family and fathered a daughter named Kadara Calnys. However, while she was only an infant, his daughter was kidnapped by the First Order. Six years later, he embarked on a quest to the desert planet of Pasaana with Kael Nerith to find answers about the growing darkness in the Synth Flux, but failed to uncover anything. Seeking to bury the pain of those memories, Calnys settled on Pasaana and led a solitary lifestyle where he was known as the "Hermit" to the Aki-Aki of the Lurch Canyon. He lived alone for some years while keeping contact with the Rennel family and was later asked by Corolan's Resistance for his aid in the war against the First Order.

In 35 ARE, Vero helped the Resistance and reunited with his old friend, Rugor. Calnys played one more role in the fight for galactic freedom as he brought an assembled Citizens' Fleet to reinforce the Resistance at the battle at Exegol, from which the Resistance emerged victorious.

A member of the tragic Calnys family, Landonis Balthazar Calnys, otherwise known as "Vero" Calnys, was born on the planet Socorro. During his youth, he became a smuggler and a gambler, excelling in a card game known as sabacc. He traveled throughout the galaxy and piloted a freighter called the Vagabond, which he won through a game of sabacc at the gas giant Bespin. The ship saved his life on numerous occasions. Sometime before 10 BRE, Vero won a subtropical moon in the Oseon Belt during a game of sabacc. Vero also developed a partnership with L3-37, a custom self-made piloting automaton.

During his time with L3, Vero nearly lost her to a Vorga after making a bad bet in a game of sabacc on Gonda. At one point, Calnys was involved in an incident with a Dawinian and their companion. He also came to meet the Tonnika sisters, even showing Brea Tonnika how to work the controls on the Falcon. At another point, Brea and Senni Tonnika made a mess of the room they were staying in on the city-world of Cintara in order to get back at Calnys. At some point, Calnys met a automaton mechanic who worked on the Mid Rim planet Kijimi, with that mechanic coming to owe Calnys a favor.

Sometime prior to the Battle of Kholar, Vero and L3-37 were ambushed in the Halthor sector when an Dominion interdictor dropped out from slipspace. As a result of the ambush, Calnys lost a majority of his shipment of walkers that he acquired from an Er'Kit arms dealer in the Noonian sector. Vero also had dealings with Crimson Dawn on Felucia. He completed his debt to Crimson Dawn and its leader, Dryden Vos. At another point, Calnys visited Pantora and successfully convinced Dominion Vice Grand Administrator Prita Sven to let him leave the planet with a freighter full of poached forlyn carcasses.

Calnys and L3-37 at some point was arrested for smuggling and was sent to the planet Hynestia. Vero and L3 was asked by Queen Forsythia Jin of Hynestia to deliver an artifact to the Iron Dominion for making up for a gherlian fur failed delivery. Princess Rinetta Gan knew about the real purpose of the artifact from her teacher Zel Gris. It was supposed to function as an energy source and make up for the lack of sunlight on the planet Livno III. Without it, Livno III would fall to ruin and destruction. Rinetta tried to force Calnys and L3 to comply with her new orders after sneaking onto the Vagabond as a stowaway. Her plan failed as Calnys traveled to Neral's moon to settle a debt with Ne'eda Frip. When the exchange went wrong and escaped but he was captured by Queen Forsythia again. Queen Forsythia put Calnys and L3 in jail. Rinetta freed Calnys and L3 again. She wanted them to help her return the artifact to Livno III. They all escaped Hynestia and had to defeat Ne'eda's starships before making the jump to Livno III. After returning the artifact to Livno III, they put the princess Rinetta back to Hynestia. Queen Forsythia pardoned Calnys and L3 afterwards.
