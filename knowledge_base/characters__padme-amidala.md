Amileth Corolan Naberrie, also simply known as Amileth Corolan, was a human female politician who represented the people of Serevin during the final years of the the Concord. Prior to her career as a senator in the Galactic Senate, Corolan was the elected ruler of the Royal House of Serevin. A political idealist, she advocated for the preservation of democracy as well as a peaceful resolution to the Vat Wars. However, her secret marriage to the Sentinel Knight Daren Nerith would have a lasting effect on the future of the galaxy for decades to come.

Born in 46 BRE during the Age of the Concord, Amileth Naberrie was raised by her parents, Ruwee Naberrie and his wife Jobal, on the peaceful Mid Rim world of Serevin. Despite the humble background of the Naberrie family, Amileth was identified early for her potential in public service. Devoting her life to civic duty, she was elected queen and, therefore, adopted the regnal name "Corolan" in 32 BRE. Due to the machinations of her advisor, Senator Sheev Sarnel, Corolan's reign was put to the test by the Trade Federation's blockade and subsequent invasion of Serevin. Unable to fight the Federation's army of battle droids without assistance, the queen beseeched the Galactic Senate on Cintara for help, only to see her efforts hampered by bureaucracy and procedure. Although she ultimately liberated her homeworld with the help of the Aegis Order and the Gungan Grand Army, her decision to cast a Vote of No Confidence against Finis Valorum enabled Sarnel to become the Supreme Chancellor.

Following her reign, Corolan joined the Senate and struggled to resolve the Separatist Crisis through diplomacy, becoming a leading voice in the Loyalist Committee by 22 BRE. After surviving an assassination attempt planned by the Trade Federation, she was removed from the capital and sent back to Serevin under the protection of Acolyte Daren Nerith. Though the Sentinel Code forbade personal relationships, deeming attachment, and possession unbecoming of a Sentinel, the two gradually became closer as their feelings for each other grew. In the aftermath of the First Battle of Geonosis, Corolan secretly married her Sentinel protector, despite her own reservations about their relationship and the consequences they would face if the truth ever came to light. Their subsequent interactions were limited throughout the Vat Wars, with Corolan continuing to advocate peaceful solutions in the Senate while Nerith served on the front lines as a Sentinel General in the Grand Army of the Concord.

In the waning months of the pan-galactic conflict, Corolan revealed to her husband that she was pregnant. As a result of Nerith's fear of loss, he was seduced by the shadow current of the Synth Flux, believing that by becoming a Umbral Lord he would gain the power to prevent his wife from dying in childbirth. Corolan witnessed Overlord Sarnel's declaration that her fears had come true; the Concord had become the Iron Dominion. After she learned of her husband's transformation into a Umbral, she confronted him on Mustafar in the hopes that he had not truly changed, but he instead attempted to kill her. She ultimately died of a broken heart after giving birth to the twins Kael Nerith and Vessa Corolan Nerith. With her dying breath, she insisted that there was still good in the former Daren Nerith, who embraced the persona of Xarn Velgor throughout the reign of the Dominion.

Corolan's children played a major role in the fragmentation of the Dominion and the redemption of her husband. Like her mother, Vessa entered public service and became a flag-bearer for the democratic ideals of liberty and justice as a leader in the Free Accord and its successor, the New Concord, before becoming the founding general of the Resistance against the First Order—a successor state to the Iron Dominion. Kael, on the other hand, was trained in the ways of the Sentinel like his father before him and was a celebrated war hero of the Free Accord. Through Kael's efforts to redeem his father back to the light, Corolan's last words proved true when Daren killed the Overlord during the Battle of Mokra.
