# Battle of Kholar

The Battle of Kholar, also known as the Battle of the Void Core or the Miracle of Kholar, was a major and decisive battle of the Sundering War, in which the Free Accord was able to destroy the Void Core and was seen as one of the first major victories over the Iron Dominion.

During the Battle of Scarif, Jyn Erso and Cassian Andor managed to steal detailed plans and design specifications for the Dominion battle station known as the Void Core and transmit them to the Alliance Fleet above; these plans soon made their way to Princess Vessa Corolan. Dominion forces under the command of the Dark Lord of the Umbral, Xarn Velgor, soon captured Princess Vessa and the crew of the Tantive IV.

Princess Vessa was taken prisoner aboard the Void Core by Lord Velgor. The Princess was rescued from captivity by an unlikely alliance of the smuggler Jax Rennel, former Sentinel Master Oben Marase, a young farmboy from Durn named Kael Nerith, and Rennel's First Mate, the Grovaki named Rugor. The group, which also included Free Accord droids Ardu and Ceevo, in possession of the Void Core plans, fled to the Accord base on Kholar 4—closely pursued by the battle station.

Before Corolan arrived, Chancellor Vela Torne was evacuated aboard a shuttle, as General Dodonna considered her the core of the rebellion. During her time away, she composed a contingency plan: surrender to Overlord Sarnel.

General Jan Dodonna briefed the accord pilots on the plan. The Void Core's defenses were designed for a direct, large-scale assault. By using small, one-man starfighters, the Rebels would slip through the Void Core's main defenses, maneuver down a narrow trench leading to the exhaust port, and fire proton torpedoes into the exhaust port. An analysis of the plans identified a weakness in the battle station, placed there by Dominion scientist Galen Erso: a small thermal exhaust port leading to the main reactor. A proton torpedo fired into the port would cause a chain reaction that would destroy the battle station.

With the Void Core approaching, Y-wings from Gold Squadron and X-wings from Red Squadron and Green Squadron were dispatched to carry out the attack plan. A total of 30 ships were deployed in the assault on the Void Core: 8 Y-wings from Gold Squadron as well as 22 X-wings, 12 from Red Squadron and the other 10 from Green Squadron. The starfighters weaved through the turret emplacements, destroying many as they passed. Red Six (Jek Tono Porkins) was the first to be destroyed in the volley of turret fire. The deployment of Dominion TIE fighters, including one piloted by Lieutenant Iden Versio, devastated the Accord attackers, destroying them far faster and more efficiently than the station's weapon emplacements had been able to. Red Four (John D. Branon) was the first to be shot down by the incoming TIEs.

During the battle, Rebels in the temple, including pilots who were left behind watched the battle from the command center, along with Fighter Boss Nera Kase. She tallied the Accord starfighter losses as they occurred during the battle.

Some time into the battle, Dominion officer Jude Edivon aboard the Void Core identified the threat of the Rebels' attack strategy, and attempted to warn her superiors of the danger. However, High Warden Varkis ignored the warning and refused to evacuate. Informed by Lieutenant Tanbris that the Rebels were avoiding the turbolaser fire, Velgor himself decided to deploy a squadron of TIE fighters to destroy the remaining Accord fighters. This would be the only Dominion squadron launched during the battle. Velgor himself would join the battle, entering the fray aboard his personal TIE Advanced as Black Leader. He was joined by two other members of Black Squadron, the TIE pilot aces "Backstabber" and Black Two Mithel, as his hand-picked wingmen.

The first attack run was performed by a trio of Y-wings from Gold Squadron. The three Y-wings, led by Gold Leader (Jon Vander) with Gold Two (Dex Tiree) and Gold Five (Davish Krail) escorting, attempted to make a shot on the exhaust port but were shot down by Velgor. A second trench run was performed by a trio of X-wings from Red Squadron flown by Red Leader (Garven Dreis), Red Ten (Theron Nett), and Red Twelve (Puck Naeco). Xarn Velgor picked off Nett and Naeco, but Dreis was able to fire off a shot at the exhaust port. However, his proton torpedoes missed their mark, only scratching the surface. Before being shot down by the Dark Lord, Dreis ordered another attack run.

Following the final command of Red Leader, Red Five (Kael Nerith) led the final attempt with Red Two (Wedge Antilles) and Red Three, Kael's old friend, (Biggs Darklighter), as wingmen. Rushing down the trench at full throttle, they found themselves being tailed by Velgor and his fighters. One of Velgor's wingmen damaged Antilles' fighter, forcing him to disengage, whilst Velgor personally destroyed Darklighter's Talon fighter, forcing Nerith to attempt the shot on his own. In this dire moment the Accord leaders were concerned when the young pilot deactivated his targeting system for the attempt, but Nerith assured them that nothing was wrong; he was advised by the spirit of Marase to rely on the Synth Flux instead for his attack.
