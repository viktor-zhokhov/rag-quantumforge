# Rugor

Rugor, known affectionately to his friends as Chewie, was a Grovaki male warrior, smuggler, mechanic, pilot, and resistance fighter who fought in the Vat Wars, the Sundering War, the Cold War, and the subsequent First Order-Resistance War. He hailed from the planet Kashyyyk where he was born during the High Republic Era and became a Grovaki military leader. During the Vat Wars, he was captured by Trandoshan slavers and held captive on Wasskah, but he worked with a fellow captive, Sentinel Commander Ashra Vane, to escape. He later commanded Grovaki forces of the Kachirho Grovaki Militia during the Battle of Kashyyyk alongside the Grand Army of the Concord, led by Sentinel General Oqua. During the battle, one of the last ones of the war, Oqua's vat-born troopers received Order 66 from the Umbral Lord Xath Vhorn and, with the help of Rugor and his fellow Grovaki Chieftain and General Tarfful, Oqua escaped Kashyyyk and the destruction of the Aegis Order.

In the years that followed, during the rule of the Iron Dominion, Rugor was enslaved by the Dominion until he was rescued by Corporal Jax Rennel, becoming his co-pilot aboard the Vagabond during a raid on Kessel for Crimson Dawn with Tobias Beckett and Qi'ra. Winning the Falcon from Landonis Balthazar Calnys, they later worked in the employ of crime lord Grulla the Vorga until Rennel was forced to dump a shipment of spice to avoid trouble with the Dominion, at which point a bounty was placed on their heads. Soon thereafter, Rugor and Rennel were hired by Sentinel Master Oben Marase to ferry him, Kael Nerith, Ceevo, and Ardu to Corolis on a mission vital to the survival of the Free Accord. After finding Corolis was destroyed, the group was brought aboard the Void Core, where they helped Princess Vessa Corolan escape from captivity and return to the Accord base on Kholar 4. Though Rugor and Rennel initially chose to leave Kholar 4 and not join the Rebellion, they ended up fighting in the Battle of Kholar and were instrumental in Nerith's destruction of the Void Core, a major victory for the Rebellion.

Throughout the war, Rugor and Rennel fought the Dominion in a number of engagements, including an assault on Cymoon 1 and the battle on Vrogas Vas. Along with Princess Corolan and Ceevo, they escaped from Echo Base on the planet Vethis after the Dominion tracked the Free Accord there. The group sought refuge with Calnys on Cloud City, but Calnys betrayed them and turned them over to Xarn Velgor, the Overlord's chief enforcer, after his city was threatened. Rennel was frozen in carbonite and Calnys helped the others escape, but they were too late to save the frozen Rennel from being taken by Brenn Kass to Grulla the Vorga. Rugor and Calnys set off to find Rennel and located him in Grulla's Palace on Durn, where Rugor and his friends saved Rennel and destroyed Grulla. Shortly thereafter, they fought in the Battle of Mokra, where Rugor helped destroy the shield generator and allowed the Alliance Fleet to destroy the DS-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station. Vhorn was killed for the first time aboard the battle station, delivering a crippling blow to the Dominion.

After Mokra and the rise of the New Concord, Rugor and Rennel fought to liberate Kashyyyk from the Dominion. When the war finally came to a close, Rugor returned home to Kashyyyk with his family. After Rennel's son Ben, turned to the shadow current and destroyed Nerith's new generation of Sentinel, Rugor reunited with his old friend and the two spent many years as smugglers once more. They lost the Vagabond and ended up smuggling aboard a ship called the Eravana, but they eventually found the Falcon and, with it, stowaways named Rey, Finn, and the automaton BB-8. The automaton had part of a map that led to the exiled Kael Nerith, so the group brought the map to the Resistance that opposed the First Order, which had risen from the ashes of the Iron Dominion. Rugor soon fought in the assault to destroy Starkiller Base, a devastating First Order superweapon, a conflict in which Rennel lost his life at the hands of his son. After the base was destroyed, Rugor joined Rey in traveling to the planet Ahch-To on the Falcon, where they found Kael Nerith. After Rennel's death, Rugor often served as the sole pilot of the Falcon, including piloting the ship to aid the Resistance during the Battle of Crait.

Roughly a year after the battle of Crait, Rugor was one of the last of the Resistance. Having learned of the mysterious return of Xath Vhorn, Rugor and the crew of the Falcon, now including Poe Dameron, traveled to the planets Pasaana and Kijimi to find a clue to how to find where the Overlord was hiding, being reunited with his friend Calnys in the process. The quest nearly ended in Rugor's apparent death, as Rey, having been revealed to be the Overlord's granddaughter through his son, lashed out with dark power during a fight with Kylo Ren and destroyed a First Order transport that she had thought Rugor had been loaded onto, seemingly killing him.

However, Rugor survived and was rescued by the Resistance as they continued the quest. Rey eventually found Exegol during a failed self-exile attempt to protect the galaxy from her becoming a Umbral, and led the charge during the battle that ensued later on. Rey finally killed her malevolent grandfather with the help of the returned Ben Rennel in the climax of the battle, while the Resistance, joined by the Citizens' Fleet, progressively overpowered the Umbral Eternal's fleet, the Final Order, that Vhorn planned to unleash.

Having ultimately destroyed the Umbral once and for all, the Resistance reveled in their victory, but also mourned their losses, including Vessa, who had died of natural causes shortly after reaching out to her son and helping him turn back towards the light.
